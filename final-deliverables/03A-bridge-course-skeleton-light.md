### Kahaani (25 min) — Concept Summary Only

**Anchor concept: The Blood Report Metaphor**
- A company's financial statements = its blood report
- P&L = performance (how the business did)
- Balance Sheet = position (what it owns and owes)
- Cash Flow = circulation (is actual cash moving?)
- When a financial statement is wrongly prepared: consequences for all stakeholders (Byju's example — wrong revenue recognition, ₹90,000 fee booked in Year 1 instead of spread across 3 years)

### Koncept — Foundation Concepts (35 min)

**Reference:** Foundation Ch 1, Units 1 and 2

**1. What is Accounting? (5 min)**
- AICPA 1961 definition: art of recording, classifying, summarising, interpreting
- Modern extension: communicating economic information for decision-making
- 6 steps in sequence: Identify → Record → Classify → Summarise → Analyse → Communicate

**2. Key Accounting Concepts — 6 Selected (25 min)**

| Concept | Core Point |
|---|---|
| Entity Concept | Business is legally and financially separate from its owner |
| Going Concern | Entity assumed to continue indefinitely; assets valued at cost, not liquidation |
| Accrual Concept | Revenue/expense recognised when earned/incurred, not when cash moves |
| Matching Concept | Expenses of a period matched with revenues of the same period |
| Dual Aspect | Every transaction has two equal and opposite effects; Assets = Liabilities + Equity |
| Conservatism | Anticipate no future profits; provide for all possible losses |

**3. Capital vs Revenue Expenditure — Brief (5 min)**
- Capital expenditure: benefit beyond one accounting period → Balance Sheet
- Revenue expenditure: benefit within one accounting period → P&L

### Koncept — CA Inter Level (35 min)

**Reference:** CA Inter Ch 1 (Introduction to AS) + Ch 2 (Framework — elements section)

**Why Accounting Standards? (10 min)**
- 3 benefits: standardisation, additional disclosures, comparability
- AS cannot override statute (if conflict, statute wins)
- 29 AS issued; 27 currently in force (AS 6 — Depreciation, AS 8 — R&D: both withdrawn)
- Two tracks in India: Old AS (most companies) + Ind AS (listed + large companies, IFRS-converged)
- IFRS: issued by IASB (formed 2001); predecessor IASC formed 1973
- India: ASB constituted 1977 under ICAI

**Framework — 5 Elements (15 min)**

| Element | Definition (one line) |
|---|---|
| Asset | Resource controlled by entity from past events; future economic benefits expected to flow IN |
| Liability | Present obligation from past events; settlement causes resources to flow OUT |
| Equity | Residual interest in assets after deducting liabilities (A − L = E) |
| Income | Increase in economic benefits → increases equity (asset inflow or liability decrease) |
| Expense | Decrease in economic benefits → decreases equity (asset outflow or liability increase) |

**Recognition Criteria (5 min)**
An element is recognised in financial statements only when both conditions are met:
1. Probable that future economic benefit will flow to/from the entity
2. The item has a cost or value that can be measured reliably

*Application: Contingent liability — condition 1 fails (not probable) → not recognised, only disclosed*

**4 Measurement Bases — Brief (5 min)**
- Historical Cost: what you paid for it
- Current Cost: what it would cost to replace today
- Realisable Value: what you can sell it for now
- Present Value: future inflows discounted to today
