## How to read this planner

- **Phase:** Grouped chapter blocks for chapter tests
- **Seq:** Teaching sequence number from the JSON curriculum file
- **Chapter:** Short chapter name
- **Day Focus:** What is covered in that 2.5-hour session (Kahaani → Koncept → Karma)
- **Notes:** Holidays, tests, Zoom sessions, special flags

**Line count basis for day allocation:**
Days per chapter are allocated proportionally to ICAI material line count (from `combined_all_chapters.md`) and exam frequency (number of attempts in 15 papers). Heavier chapters (by volume + exam weight) get more days. Zero-frequency chapters are kept lean.
