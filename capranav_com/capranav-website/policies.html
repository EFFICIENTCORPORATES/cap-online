Privacy Policy

This static website does not require an account for reading articles or using the path selector. Saved guides, completed sections and selector choices are stored locally in the visitor’s browser. They are not transmitted by this static package. External services opened through links apply their own privacy policies.

04

Terms of Use

Content is provided for personal educational use. Students remain responsible for verifying the applicable syllabus, attempt, examination notification and official requirements. Do not use the website to distribute unlawful material, interfere with functionality or misrepresent the content as official ICAI communication.

05

Refund and Cancellation Policy

All purchases made directly on this website — the course, individual chapters and printed books — are final. We do not offer refunds or cancellations once an order is placed. Where a course is purchased through an external enrollment provider instead, that provider's own refund and cancellation terms apply.

06

Course Access Policy

For courses and chapters purchased directly on this website, access details are sent to the email or WhatsApp number provided at checkout, typically within 24 hours of payment. Access is personal and must not be shared or resold. For a course purchased through an external enrollment provider, validity, device limits, login rules, class availability and extension conditions are governed by that provider's own enrollment terms. Website articles and free resources do not by themselves create enrollment rights.

06A

Shipping Policy

Printed books are packed and dispatched from our own warehouse through our courier partner, at the all-inclusive price shown at checkout — no separate shipping charge is added. Only prepaid orders are accepted; Cash on Delivery is not available. Delivery address, phone number and email are collected at checkout — please double-check these before paying, as they're used directly for dispatch. A tracking update follows once the order ships.

They may not be republished, sold, substantially copied or presented as another person’s work without permission. Third-party and official resources remain subject to the rights and conditions of their respective owners.

08

Disclaimer

Educational guidance cannot guarantee marks, rank, passing or a particular result. Student outcomes depend on prior knowledge, attendance, practice, revision, health and examination performance. For ICAI updates and technical requirements, the official ICAI notification remains authoritative.

09

Grievance and escalation

For course-access, payment or enrollment grievances, first use the support route provided on the purchase confirmation so the correct transaction can be identified. If a concern relates specifically to content on this website, contact CA Pranav through an official profile with the page title, issue and supporting screenshot. Do not include confidential credentials.
