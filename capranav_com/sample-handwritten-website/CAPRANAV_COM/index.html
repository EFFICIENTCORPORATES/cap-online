The Author’s Journey

A result was never promised.
The work was.

What began in a small Kathmandu classroom became two All India Rank 1 results, AIR 5 in CA Final, professional experience across audit, industry and public policy—and finally a student-first way of teaching Accounts.

“I am not guaranteeing the result. I am guaranteeing the work.”
Class 11The decision in Kathmandu

He chose CA, publicly declared that he would pursue a rank and built discipline before certainty arrived.
