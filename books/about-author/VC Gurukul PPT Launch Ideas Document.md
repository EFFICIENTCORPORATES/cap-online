## About This Document

This document has been prepared as an internal planning reference for the launch of **Pranav Pratik Tulshyan** as the new Accounts faculty at **VC Gurukul**.

Pranav is a double All India Rank 1 holder (CPT & IPCC) and AIR 5 in CA Finals — making him one of the most decorated CA rankers to enter the teaching profession. This launch is being planned across VC Gurukul's existing digital ecosystem — Instagram, YouTube (35K subscribers), Telegram, and WhatsApp communities — with a minimal paid budget and a maximum organic content strategy.

The purpose of this document is to list every possible content idea, story angle, reel concept, and activity that can be executed across the pre-launch and launch period — week by week — so the team has a single reference point to plan, assign, and execute from.

This is a **brainstorming and planning document.** Not every idea needs to be executed. The team should shortlist 15–20 ideas that are realistic to produce and impactful enough to move the needle.

## Core Campaign Philosophy

> *"We are not launching a faculty. We are introducing a person."*

Every piece of content created for this launch serves one purpose — answering the single question in every CA student's mind:

**"Why should I trust this person with the most important exam of my life?"**

The answer is not in the credentials alone. It is in the story. Pranav's journey — from a boy in Kathmandu raising his hand while people laughed, to a double AIR 1 holder who survived two earthquakes and a national blockade to get there — is the campaign. Every reel, every post, every quote is one more chapter of that story being told.

## Content Ideas — Week by Week

---

### PRE-LAUNCH — Internal Community First
*Before anything goes public. Warm up the existing student base.*

1. **Rahul Arora's handover video** — A warm, personal 2–3 minute video from Rahul to existing students on Telegram and WhatsApp. He explains his departure, introduces Pranav genuinely, and passes the baton. This is the single most trust-building move of the entire launch.

2. **Seed the conversation in existing classes** — All three founders casually mention "someone new is coming for accounts" in their own sessions. No formal announcement. Just a natural reference that plants curiosity.

3. **Pranav's 60-second intro video — internal only** — Raw, unproduced. Just him on camera saying who he is and why he's here. Sent to Telegram community before any public post.

4. **"Something is coming" pin on Telegram** — A single graphic with just the numbers: AIR 1 | AIR 1 | AIR 5. No name. No explanation. Pin it and let curiosity do the work.

---

### WEEK 1 — The Credentials Drop
*Goal: Make his name known. Let the numbers speak first.*

5. **The stark credentials graphic** — AIR 1. AIR 1. AIR 5. Clean design, minimal text. Posted on Instagram and YouTube Community Tab. No caption needed beyond the numbers.

6. **"First in 70 years" post** — Standalone post on the historic significance: first foreign student in ICAI's 70+ year history to achieve AIR 1. Most CA students do not know this. It will stop the scroll.

7. **Academic timeline carousel** — A multi-slide Instagram carousel: Class 10 → Class 12 → CPT AIR 1 → IPCC AIR 1 → EY → IOCL → Ministry → VC Gurukul. One milestone per slide. Clean and sequential.

8. **"Who is he?" Instagram story series** — A 10-slide Instagram story that reveals one fact per slide over 24 hours. Builds curiosity progressively. Ends with his name and the batch start date.

9. **Text-on-screen reel — ranks only** — A reel with no face, no voice. Just ranks appearing on screen one by one with dramatic pacing. Let the facts land without commentary.

10. **Member & Student Registration Number post** — Posting his ICAI registration numbers alongside his credentials. Niche but highly credible — serious CA students immediately recognise what these mean and how to verify them.

11. **Corporate credentials post** — Separate post on his professional life: EY → IOCL (₹9L Cr revenue company) → Ministry of Petroleum. Positions him not just as a ranker but as someone who has lived the real world of finance.

---

### WEEK 2 — The Human Story
*Goal: Make him real. Convert admiration into emotional connection.*

12. **The raised hand reel** — Pranav on camera, telling the story of raising his hand in the Kathmandu classroom while people mocked him. 60–90 seconds, raw, direct. Ends with: *"I am not guaranteeing the result. I am guaranteeing the work."*

13. **Quote graphic — the guarantee** — *"I am not guaranteeing the result. I am guaranteeing the work."* — Standalone graphic. No context needed. This line speaks for itself.

14. **The PCO booth story** — Reel or long-form caption: a boy in Kathmandu walks to a PCO booth for 3–4 days to call a stranger — a CA ranker — just to find out if AIR 1 is possible for a normal student. What he heard in that 80-second call changed everything.

15. **Quote graphic — on the rank** — *"The rank was not the achievement. The confidence it gave me for a lifetime — that was the achievement."*

16. **The earthquake story** — Reel or long caption: how a massive earthquake hit Kathmandu in April 2015 during his IPCC preparation. 25–30 days of study lost. Roads cracking, buildings falling, survival mode. And he still aimed for AIR 1.

17. **The Nepal blockade story** — How he went to his CA Inter exams by cramming into shared tempos during a national fuel crisis when petrol was ₹500/litre and public transport had nearly collapsed.

18. **"From Kathmandu to Noida" reel** — A simple, visual journey reel of his geographic and professional path. Kathmandu → Delhi (EY) → IOCL → Ministry → VC Gurukul. Cinematic and human.

19. **Quote graphic — on obstacles** — *"Be stubborn about your goals. Be flexible about your methods."* — With a one-line context referencing the earthquake as the moment this became real for him.

20. **The Eklavya philosophy post** — His belief that CA is fundamentally a self-study course and a teacher's real job is to give direction — not carry the student. A reel or graphic built around the Eklavya reference.

21. **The Steve Jobs quote — in context** — The quote *"The only way to do great work is to love what you do"* — but told with the context that this is what he said on Day 1 of CPT class when his teacher asked every student for their personal tagline. The context makes it entirely different from a generic quote post.

22. **Quote graphic — Gita verse** — *"Karmanya Vadhikaraste Ma Phaleshu Kadachana"* — with his interpretation: you can guarantee the work, never the result. Resonates deeply with CA students who understand exam pressure.

---

### WEEK 3 — The Teaching Proof
*Goal: Let students experience the teaching before they pay. Build trust through content.*

23. **AS-11 full video on YouTube** — His first full-length teaching video. Position it as the definitive explanation of one of CA Inter's most feared topics. This is the centrepiece of Week 3.

24. **Deferred Tax full video on YouTube** — The second full-length video. Position it as: *"The topic everyone fears, explained once and for all."*

25. **Short reel cut from the long video** — A 2–3 minute Instagram reel extracted from the YouTube video. One concept, one clean explanation. Drives traffic to the full video.

26. **"Why does AS-11 confuse everyone?" reel** — A question-based hook reel where Pranav diagnoses the most common student mistake before teaching the concept. Problem-first teaching hooks students immediately.

27. **Real world vs CA syllabus reel** — How this accounting standard actually shows up in a company like IOCL where he worked for years. Connects the syllabus to reality in a way no textbook can.

28. **Pranav posts on his personal YouTube channel** — A personal video on Pranav Pratik Tulsian channel: why he is making this transition to teaching. Honest, unscripted, direct. Brings his personal audience into the VC Gurukul ecosystem.

29. **The 60-second concept reel** — Pranav explains something genuinely complex in under 60 seconds. Demonstrates his ability to simplify without dumbing down.

30. **"What CA Inter Accounts actually tests" reel** — His perspective on what ICAI really wants from a student's answer — not memorisation, not volume, but understanding and structure.

31. **The summary-making strategy reel** — His IPCC preparation strategy of making hand-written summaries for every question so the entire subject can be revised in one day. Pure value content. No selling. Students will share this.

32. **"What EY taught me that no coaching class did" reel** — Audit perspective on accounts. What real-world assurance work teaches you about financial statements that the syllabus alone never can.

33. **"What working at IOCL taught me about accounts" reel** — Real corporate finance perspective: budgeting, consolidation, MIS — how these connect back to what CA Inter students are studying.

### WEEK 4 — Enrollment Opens + FOMO Phase
*Goal: Convert interested students into enrolled students. Create urgency without pressure.*

34. **Enrollment announcement post** — Clean, clear, complete: batch details, start date, fees, platform link. No clutter. Everything the student needs to make a decision in one post.

35. **Early bird pricing post** — First X seats at Y price, with a clear deadline. Scarcity is honest — the batch genuinely starts on 13th July. Use it.

36. **Rahul Arora's public endorsement** — Rahul publicly endorses Pranav on VC Gurukul's Instagram. Either a joint reel or a quote from Rahul as a designed graphic. An outgoing faculty recommending his replacement is the most credible endorsement possible.

37. **Individual founder endorsements** — Gurpreet and Rahul Bhutani each post personally about Pranav on their own accounts. Personal posts from co-founders carry more weight than institute posts.

38. **Comment and DM screenshot social proof** — Screenshots of genuine reactions from students who watched the YouTube videos. Comments, DMs, reactions. Real social proof without manufactured testimonials.

39. **Countdown posts** — 7 days to batch start. 3 days. 1 day. Simple, consistent, builds anticipation.

40. **Daily study tip stories during enrollment week** — Pranav posts one study tip per day on Instagram stories during the enrollment window. Starts building a habit of following him before the first class even begins.

47. **Pranav walks into the classroom** — Simple, cinematic. No dialogue. Just him entering the classroom for the first time. The visual alone tells the story.

> *"I am not guaranteeing the result. I am guaranteeing the work."*
— Said publicly when peers mocked him for targeting AIR 1 in Kathmandu. The line that defines his entire philosophy.

> *"The rank was not the achievement. The feeling — and the confidence it gave me for a lifetime — that was the achievement."*

— His core learning from the 2015 Nepal Earthquake, when 30 days of IPCC preparation were lost and he had to rebuild his strategy from scratch.

> *"What you do not revise the day before the exam, you cannot write in the exam."*
— His single most important strategy for IPCC, built around the belief that revision is not optional — it is the exam itself.

> *"Keep learning. Keep sharing."*
— His personal tagline. The philosophy behind starting Efficient Corporates. The reason he is now stepping into teaching.

— His internal response when peers in Kathmandu laughed at him for raising his hand and targeting AIR 1.

— His belief on public goal-setting and the relationship between effort and self-respect.

> *"I can guarantee what I do. I can never guarantee what comes back."*
— A variation of his core philosophy, rooted in the Gita. Applicable to every CA student anxious about results.

> *"Once you have felt that something impossible is possible — that feeling stays with you for life. No one can take it away."*
— On what CPT AIR 1 truly gave him beyond the rank itself.

> *"When I talked to the AIR 3 ranker on that call, I realised — he was just a normal student. Exactly like me. That 80-second call changed everything."*
— On the PCO booth call that dismantled his belief that rank holders are different kinds of people.

> *"A teacher's job is to show you the direction. The walking is always yours to do."*
— His philosophy on the student-teacher relationship, rooted in his admiration for Eklavya.

> *"CA is a self-study course. Always has been. A good teacher makes the path clearer — but they cannot walk it for you."*
— His belief on what CA preparation really demands from a student.

> *"The top 10 rankers are all the same. Don't let a rank number fool you into thinking otherwise — not even AIR 1."*
— Said in the context of his CA Finals AIR 5, maintaining the same humility he had when he was AIR 1.

> *"He didn't just study accounts. He lived it — in a Big Four firm, in a Fortune 500 company, in a government ministry. Now he's here to teach it."*
— A third-person framing from the VC Gurukul team. Suitable for institute-authored posts.
