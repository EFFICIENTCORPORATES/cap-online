##### Illustration Solution 5

```text
(i)     New profit sharing ratio:
        A = 3/5 – 1/10 = 5/10
        B = 2/5 i.e. 4/10
        C = 1/10
        i.e. 5:4: 1
(ii)    A’s sacrifice 1/10× 2/5 = 2/50
        B’s sacrifice 1/10 × 3/5 = 3/50
        New profit sharing ratio
        A = 3/5 – 2/50 = 28/50
        B = 2/5 – 3/50 = 17/50
        C = 1/10 i.e. 5/50
        i.e. 28:17: 5
(iii)   Let total share be 1
        C’s share=1/10
        Remaining share=1-1/10=9/10
        Distribution:
        A = 9/10 × 3/5 = 27/50

        B = 9/10 × 2/5 = 18/50
        C = 1/10. i.e. = 5/50
        i.e. 27:18: 5
```
