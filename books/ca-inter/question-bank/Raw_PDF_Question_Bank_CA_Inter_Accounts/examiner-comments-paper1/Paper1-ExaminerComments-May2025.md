PAPER – 1: ADVANCED ACCOUNTING  
Specific Comments  
Question 1 .(a) A significant number of examinees displayed a lack of 
understanding of the provisions of AS 10 “Property, Plant and Equipment ”. 
Instead of applying the correct provisions, many examinees  incorrectly based 
their responses on AS 16 – Borrowing Costs, especial ly while commenting on 
the capitalisation of expenses related to the relocation of production facilities. 
Additionally, several examine es were unable to accurately compute the cost of 
land. 
(b) Few examinees were not able to determine the correct value of
investments as on 31st March, 2025. Furthermore, the calculation of profit on
the sale of shares was often incorrect, and the treatment  of investments was
not adequately supported with reference to the relevant provisions of AS 13
“Accounting for Investments ”.
Question 2. Many examinees were unable to classify items correctly in the 
Balance Sheet  as per the Schedule III format  prescribed under the 
Companies Act, 2013 . Key adjustments such as preliminary expenses and 
profit on reissue of shares were often missed. A common error was the 
omission of the number of shares in the Notes to Accounts . Only a few 
examinees prepared the notes in the p rescribed manner.  
Question 3.(a) Several examinees made mistakes in calculating income taxes 
paid and showed a lack of conceptual clarity regarding the treatment of 
Deferred Tax Liability. Errors were also observed in computing acquisitions of 
Property, P lant and Equipment, which led to  incorrect preparation of the Cash 
Flow Statement in accordance with AS 3  “Cash Flow Statement”  for the year 
ended 31st March, 2025.  
(b) Most examinees exhibited inadequate knowledge of AS 25 '' Interim
Financial Reporting'' .  They failed to calculate t he Adjusted Profit Before Tax
for the third quarter accurately. Even among those who arrived at the correct
figure, very few provided appropriate observations with sound reasoning.

Question 4. A majority of  examinees were unabl e to correctly compute the 
purchase consideration as per AS 14  “Accounting for Amalgamations”. Many 
failed to pass the correct journal entries in the books of Pickles Ltd. Some 
wrongly used a Revaluation Account instead of the Realisa tion Account. There 
were also frequent errors in recording the sale of furniture and fixtures. 
Mistakes were commonly observed in figures related to the Retirement 
Gratuity Fund and Investment in Shares Allotment entries. Consequently, 
several examinees fa iled to prepare accur ate Realisation Accounts and Cash 
Accounts.  
Question 5.(a)  Most examinees lacked familiarity with the prescribed 
Schedule III format for the Consolidated Profit and Loss Account under the 
Companies Act, 2013, and also did not correct ly adjust for inter -company 
transactions.  
(b) Many examinees were unable to compute Goodwill or Capital Reserve as
on the date of acquisition. There was confusion regarding the treatment of
dividends – particularly whether dividends were declared from post -
acquisition profits or received for the pre -acquisition period. Several
examinees incorrectly interchanged assumptions, and many failed to adjust
the pre-acquisition dividends from the cost of investment as required.
Question 6.(a) A few examinees could not clearly explain ob jectives of 
"Accounting Standards" and advantages of setting the Accounting Standards.  
(b) Many examinees were unfamiliar with the three conditions for declaring
dividends from accumulated profits. They failed to compute the dividend
amount correctly. Some  incorrectly considered Revaluation Reserves as Fr
