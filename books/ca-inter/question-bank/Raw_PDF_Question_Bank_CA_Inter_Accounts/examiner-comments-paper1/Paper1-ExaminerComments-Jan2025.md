Specific Comments  
Question 1 .(a)  Most of the examinees  failed to calculate the weighted 
average number of shares required for determining Basic EPS . As a result, they 
were unable to compute EPS accurately in accordance with AS 20, "Earnings 
per Share."  
(b) Many examinees calculated the unearned finance income correctly, some 
failed to include the unguaranteed residual value in the t otal lease payments 
to determine the gro ss investment. Consequently, they could not ascertain the 
unearned finance income as per AS 19, "Leases."  
(c) Most of the examinees  were unable to calculate the correct amount of 
Defined Benefit Asset / Liability to  be recognized for the year.    
Question 2.  A Large number of examinees were not able to prepare the 
Balance sheet  and  Notes to accounts  in compliance with Schedule III of the 
Companies Act, 2013, using the given information. Additionally, many failed t o 
pass journal entries correctly for the se ttlement of preference shareholders 
and debenture holders' claims. As a result, they could not determine the 
correct balance for Capital Reduction/Internal Reconstruction to be 
transferred to Capital Reserve or the number of debentures issued to meet 
outstanding liabilities. The calculation of 12% Debentures required to settle 
liabilities and maintain the cash balance was also incorrect in most cases.  
Question 3.(a)  Several examinees calculated the provision amount incorrectly 
by determining the period from  the date of the invoice instead of the balance 
period. This led to errors in computing the provision amounts for both years 
as per AS 29, "Provisions, Contingent Liabilities, and Contingent Assets ." 
(b) Some examinees successfully prepared the Cash Flow S tatement. 
However, a few mistakenly added the net interest amount (after payments) 
instead of the provisioned interest while calculating cash flow from operating 
activities. This miscalculation also affected cash flow from financing activities.
