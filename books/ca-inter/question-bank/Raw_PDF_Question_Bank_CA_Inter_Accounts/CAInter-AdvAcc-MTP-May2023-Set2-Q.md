1. (a) ABC Limited has started construction of an asset on 1st December, 2021, which continues till 31st

January to March 2022. At the beginning of the year, the entity had taken Inter Corporate Deposits

on 1st March, 2022. Interest was paid on the overdraft at 10% until 1st January, 2022 and then the
rate was increased to 12%. You are required to calculate the annual capitalization rate for
computation of borrowing cost in accordance with AS 16 'Borrowing Costs'.

last 12 months upto 31.03.2021. The company now wants to change it and make provision based

120 lakhs. Provision required based on technical evaluation amounts ` 3.00 lakhs. However,

the cost of the plant during financial year 2016-2017? Assume depreciation is charged on fixed
