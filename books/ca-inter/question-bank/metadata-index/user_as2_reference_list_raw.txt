Summary of "Common Student Mistakes" identified in the Sources:
NRV Miscalculation: Forgetting that NRV is Estimated Selling Price minus Estimated Costs of Completion minus Estimated Costs to make the sale
.
Raw Materials: Writing down raw materials to replacement cost automatically. Rule: Only write them down if the finished product is expected to be sold below cost
.
Abnormal Waste: Including abnormal wastage in inventory value to "save" the P&L; this is explicitly forbidden by AS 2
.
GST/Taxes: Including recoverable taxes in the cost. Only non-refundable duties and taxes should be included
.
Standard Costs: Using standard costs that are outdated and do not approximate actual costs
.
