# 🎬 Universe Intro — Storyboard / Build Plan

> **File to build:** `universe-intro.html`
> **Purpose:** A one-time, click-to-advance introduction to the entire Accounting Universe and its 6 characters — shown once before a student starts the chapters.
> **Course:** CA Inter — Advanced Accounting · **Brand:** VC Gurukul · `#pranavBhaiya` · `#NewtonofAccounts`


`Narrator band` (neutral, framing) · `SpeechBubble` (first-person, speaker name in their colour) · `Character` (colour + accessory + emoji badge) · `ICAI Says` panel (navy + gold) · `Scene transition card` · `Chrome` (progress bar, beat counter, footer, back).

## 3. Notes / decisions baked in
- **Voice:** every character speaks **first person**; only framing lines use the neutral **Narrator band**; rules/promise use **ICAI Says**.
- **Emoji:** one badge per beat, only at a real emotional point — never spammed.
- **Length:** ~31 beats. Comfortable 2–3 min watch at a click-through pace.
- **Engine:** identical chrome to AS 2 (progress bar, beat counter `n / 31`, scene tag, footer, ← back, hash deep-link, resume). Mobile rotate-to-landscape prompt included.
- **Backgrounds:** follow the Character-Bible DO/DON'T — flat, subtle, 2–3 props max.
