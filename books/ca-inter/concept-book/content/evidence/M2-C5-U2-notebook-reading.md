**Page 17 (when depreciation stops; retirement).** Depreciation stops / ceases when: (1) residual value of asset > carrying amount (WDV) of the asset ("dep. karte karte ek din aisa aayega ki RV > WDV", then meter band); (2) at the earlier of (A) asset retired from active use and held for disposal (only retire karne se depreciation band nahi hogi), (B) asset derecognised: (i) disposal by sale, by entering into a finance lease, by donation, (ii) when no further economic benefit is expected ("asset hata denge"). Accounting treatment A: retirement: asset should be carried at **lower of carrying amount (WDV) and net realisable value (NRV)**; any write-down due to this valuation goes to P&L. Example iPad WDV 15,000: scenario 1 NRV 20,000, no write-up (the 5,000 gain is ignored); scenario 2 NRV 12,000, 3,000 low, write down to P&L. Certainty: high.

**Page 18 (derecognition; component accounting).** B Derecognise: net gain = net disposal proceeds (if any) minus carrying amount (WDV), to P&L; this is not revenue (AS 9), but other income; small exception for finance lease (AS 19). **Page 19 (component rules; depreciation to P&L).** If any part of PPE has (a) a significant portion of the total cost and (b) a different useful life, it is regarded as a separate component and depreciated separately.
