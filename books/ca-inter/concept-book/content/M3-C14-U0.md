## T1 · Meaning of reconstruction {#M3-C14-U0-T1}

**Concept**
- Loss-making company: assets overstated, fictitious assets, P&L debit → over-capitalised.
- Reconstruction: revalue assets, reassess liabilities, write off losses, cut paid-up value, vary rights.
- Aim: save the company without winding up.
- **Internal:** same company continues; Sections 61 and 66; court/tribunal confirmation; capital cut, outsiders may sacrifice.
- **External:** old company liquidated, new one formed, same shareholders; Section 232; no capital cut; = amalgamation in the nature of merger (AS 14).

**Exam algorithm: which type?**
1. Is the old company liquidated?
2. Is capital reduced?
3. Not liquidated + reduced → internal. Liquidated + new company → external.

**Exam points**
- Reconstruction ≠ closing the company.
- Internal: Sec 61, 66. External: Sec 232 + AS 14.

**Stories:** S1, S3, S4 · **Where ICAI tests it:** TYK MCQ 1, 3, 4, 6, 7.

**Linkages**
- ICAI Chapter 14 says external reconstruction is covered under amalgamation in the nature of merger: see AS 14, Amalgamation in the Nature of Merger (M2-C9-U2-T2.4), and Chapter 13 (M3-C13-U0). AS 14's own text does not use the word reconstruction (checked), so the connection is stated only in Chapter 14.

**Remember** *(agent draft)*: Company bimaar hai, band nahi karni. Internal mein wahi company; external mein nayi.

## T2 · Methods of internal reconstruction {#M3-C14-U0-T2}

**Concept**
- Five methods, often together: alteration of share capital; variation of rights; reduction of share capital; compromise/arrangement; surrender of shares.

**Exam algorithm: which methods use the Capital Reduction A/c?**
1. Alteration → no.
2. Variation → no.
3. Reduction, compromise, surrender (and unrepresented reserves) → yes.

**Exam points**
- Theory answer = five headings. Heading 1 covers sub-division/consolidation + stock conversion.
- Alteration and variation: nothing lost, so no account.

**Stories:** S2 · **Where ICAI tests it:** TYK Theory Q11.

**Remember** *(agent draft)*: Pehle do mein paisa wahi; baaki teen mein balidaan.

## T3 · Alteration of share capital {#M3-C14-U0-T3}

**Concept (Sec 61)**
- Increase authorised capital; consolidate/sub-divide; shares ↔ stock; cancel untaken shares.
- Sub-division/consolidation: paid-up proportion stays; total capital unchanged; only face value and share count change.
- Stock: fully paid shares only; no share count.

**Exam algorithm**
1. New face value and new share count from the ratio; recount.
2. Entry: old class Dr, new class Cr.
3. Only paid-up (called-up) amount; never authorised or uncalled.
4. Stock: fully paid only; show without count; recount on re-conversion.

**Exam points**
- No Capital Reduction A/c.
- Some pass no entry as the amount is unchanged.

**Stories:** none yet · **Where ICAI tests it:** Illustrations 1 (B Ltd) and 2 (C Ltd); first step of Illustration 7 and TYK Q17; conversion in TYK Q14.

**Linkages:** alteration (Sec 61) loses nothing; reduction (Sec 66, T5) writes off a loss.

**Remember** *(agent draft)*: Sirf label badla, paisa wahi.

## T4 · Variation of shareholders' rights {#M3-C14-U0-T4}

**Concept (Sec 48)**
- Change the rights of a class (dividend rate, cumulative → non-cumulative); amount unchanged.
- Entry: old description Dr, new description Cr.

**Exam points**
- Amount same → nothing lost → no Reconstruction A/c.
- Usually part of a bigger scheme with preference shares (arrears, conversion).

**Stories:** S2 · **Where ICAI tests it:** only inside schemes, e.g. Illustrations 4 and 6.

**Linkages:** arrears and exchange of preference shares: T6.

**Remember** *(agent draft)*: Rights badle, amount nahi.

## T5 · Reduction of share capital {#M3-C14-U0-T5}

**Concept (Sec 66: special resolution + tribunal)**
- (a) Cancel unpaid liability on shares.
- (b) Pay off excess paid-up capital.
- (c) Cancel lost or unrepresented capital.
- Only (c) is a sacrifice → credit Capital Reduction A/c.
- (c) forms: cut paid-up value only, or cut nominal and paid-up value.

**Exam algorithm**
1. Which mode?
2. (a): relabel partly paid as fully paid at the lower amount.
3. (b): old capital Dr; new capital Cr; refund via Sundry Shareholders A/c, then bank.
4. (c): old capital Dr; new capital Cr; difference Cr to Capital Reduction A/c.

**Exam points**
- Sundry Shareholders A/c = mode (b) only.
- ICAI is inconsistent: text says Tribunal, MCQ 5 key says Court. In the MCQ, choose the key.

**Stories:** S2 · **Where ICAI tests it:** TYK MCQ 5, MCQ 8; reductions inside Illustrations 3, 4, 5, 6, 8.

**Remember** *(agent draft)*: Teen mode: unpaid maaf, zyada paisa wapas, lost capital cancel. Balidaan sirf teesre mein.

## T6 · Compromise and arrangements {#M3-C14-U0-T6}

**Concept**
- Agreement between company and members/outsiders; every party sacrifices.
- Shareholders give up reserves → Reserves Dr, Reconstruction A/c Cr.
- Liability settled for less → liability Dr (sacrifice), Reconstruction A/c Cr.

**Exam algorithm: one party at a time**
1. Give-up vs receive.
2. Gives up more → credit (sacrifice).
3. Receives more → debit (loss).
4. Unrecorded claims (preference arrears, contingent liability): entry only for the part settled.

**Exam points**
- **Preference dividend waived and never recorded → no entry. If anything is issued in lieu (shares, cash) → debit Capital Reduction A/c.** *(Pranav, 2026-11-13)*
- Asset handed over: claim larger than value → sacrifice; claim smaller than value → loss.
- Higher interest on new debentures → no entry.

**Stories:** S2, S3 · **Where ICAI tests it:** Illustrations 3 to 8; TYK Q13, Q14, Q17, Q18.

**Linkages**
- The contingent liability not provided for in Illustration 8 is an AS 29 contingent liability: see AS 29, Contingent Liabilities (M2-C6-U2-T2.9).

**Remember** *(agent draft)*: Jisne balidaan diya woh credit; jisne zyada le liya woh debit.

## T7 · Surrender of shares {#M3-C14-U0-T7}

**Concept**
- Sub-divide → shareholders surrender some shares → reissue to debenture holders and creditors → unused cancelled to Reconstruction A/c.

**Exam algorithm**
1. Sub-divide (T3).
2. Surrender → Share Surrender A/c.
3. Issue to claimants out of surrendered shares (not fresh).
4. Unused → Reconstruction A/c.

**Exam points**
- Share Surrender A/c appears only here.

**Stories:** S2 · **Where ICAI tests it:** Illustration 7 (Revise Limited); TYK MCQ 9.

**Remember** *(agent draft)*: Pehle surrender, phir reissue, bacha hua cancel.

## T8 · Entries in case of internal reconstruction {#M3-C14-U0-T8}

**Concept**
- **Capital Reduction A/c: credit all sacrifices (balidaan); debit what they were used for; balancing figure = Capital Reserve.** *(Pranav)*
- P&L debit is written off first with the sacrifices; if still short, use General Reserve (any reserve). *(Pranav; ICAI: reserves may be used)*
- Alteration and variation: no account.
- Balance sheet: "and Reduced" only if the court orders; show the amount written off against fixed assets for five years.

**Exam algorithm** *(my exam routine from ICAI's points; not ICAI's wording)*
1. Credits: capital cut, liabilities cut, assets appreciated, reserves given up.
2. Debits: P&L loss first, then overvalued assets and intangibles, assets given away above value, scheme expenses (if stated).
3. Revalue before transferring; write down before cash is paid.
4. Unstated write-off = balancing figure.
5. Credit balance → Capital Reserve; debit balance → reserves, shortfall only.

**Exam points**
- Never look for the unstated write-off; compute it.
- Overdraft: netted in some solutions, separate in others (see Illustrations 3 and 8).

**Stories:** S2 · **Where ICAI tests it:** every illustration and scenario question; TYK MCQ 2, MCQ 10; Q12, Q16, Q18.

**Linkages**
- Reconstructed balance sheets are drawn in the Schedule III format: see Final Accounts in Chapter 11 Unit 1 (M3-C11-U1-T1.5).
- Chapter 13 Illustration 11 (an amalgamation after a capital reduction) treats the overdraft differently from Illustration 3 here: see M3-C13-U0.

**Remember** *(Pranav's words, agent's spelling)*: Credit mein balidaan; debit mein balidaan ka use; jo bacha woh Capital Reserve.

## Illustration cards

### ILL-1 · Illustration 1, B Ltd
**Difficulty:** Easy (computed from 2 concepts)
**Goal:** entries for sub-division then consolidation; notes year by year.
**Concepts:** sub-division and consolidation [T3]; entry on paid-up amount only [T3]
**Given:** part-paid shares sub-divided, later consolidated.
**Approach:** relabel the same paid-up amount (old Dr, new Cr); recount shares; restate authorised capital.
**Exam points:** no Capital Reduction A/c; entry on paid-up only; total capital unchanged.
**Tricky points:**
- **Error Register (most common):** Wrong: treat it as a reduction, open Capital Reduction A/c or pass the entry on authorised capital. Right: relabel paid-up only. Why: nothing is lost.
- **Error Register:** Wrong: carry the old share count. Right: recount from the new face value.

**Approach:** two relabelling entries; show stock without a count.
**Exam points:** fully paid shares only; no Capital Reduction A/c.

### ILL-3 · Illustration 3, A & Co. Ltd
**Difficulty:** Hard (computed from 7 concepts)
**Goal:** entries for a full scheme (with cash) and the closing balance sheet.
**Concepts:** equity and preference capital reduced [T5]; preference arrears waived in part, rest in shares [T6]; property revalued and given to debenture holders [T6]; penalty for cancelling a capital commitment [T6]; directors' loan settled in shares, cash and waiver [T6]; write-offs and balancing figure [T8]; overdraft in closing balance sheet [T8]
**Given:** four years of arrears, debentures, directors' loan, capital commitment, many write-offs.
**Approach:** take each term one by one; revalue property before handing it over; arrears: only the part settled; write-offs as the scheme says; cash entries last.
**Exam points:** arrears are not in the books; debentures reduce by the property's valuation, not book value; penalty paid is a debit; directors' loan splits three ways.
**Tricky points:**
- **Error Register (most common):** Wrong: enter the whole arrears as a liability and credit the waived part. Right: no entry for the waived part; the part settled in shares or cash is debited to Capital Reduction A/c. Why: arrears have no balance in the books.
- **Error Register:** Wrong: reduce debentures by the property's book value. Right: by its valuation after revaluing.
- **Error Register:** the overdraft. ### ILL-4 · Illustration 4, Rebuilt Ltd
**Difficulty:** Medium (computed from 4 concepts)
**Goal:** entries and balance sheet after the scheme.
**Concepts:** equity cut + new shares for cash [T5]; preference exchange with arrears [T6]; loan converted to shares [T6]; intangibles written off as the balancing figure [T8]
**Given:** both classes sacrifice; arrears cancelled for new shares; lender converts loan; right shares for cash.
**Approach:** reduction apart from fresh cash; per class: give-up vs receive; intangibles = what is left in the account.
**Exam points:** preference holders receive more than they give up → extra is a **debit**.
**Tricky points:**
- **Error Register (most common):** Wrong: hunt for the goodwill/trademark write-off in the question. Right: it is the balancing figure of Capital Reduction A/c.
- **Error Register:** Wrong: credit every exchange as a sacrifice. Right: a class paid more than it gives up gives a debit.

### ILL-5 · Illustration 5, Vaibhav Ltd
**Difficulty:** Hard (computed from 6 concepts)
**Goal:** entries and balance sheet after the scheme.
**Concepts:** equity and preference reduced to stated values [T5]; debentures exchanged at lower value [T6]; creditor forgoes part, shares for the rest [T6]; tax provision settled for more [T6]; assets written down before cash payments [T8]; surplus to Capital Reserve [T8]
**Given:** every class reduced; debentures exchanged; creditor forgoes a share; tax settled in cash.
**Approach:** each reduction credited; debentures: sacrifice; creditor: forgone part only; tax: extra is a debit; write down assets first, then pay; surplus → Capital Reserve.
**Exam points:** higher interest on new debentures → no entry; surplus not left in the account.
**Tricky points:**

- **Error Register:** Wrong: credit the creditor's whole claim. Right: only the part forgone; the rest becomes shares.
