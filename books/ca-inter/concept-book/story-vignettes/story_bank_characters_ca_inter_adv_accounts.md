## How to read this document

For every chapter (in **your teaching sequence**), you get:

- **Exam pulse** — how often it appeared in 15 attempts, so we know where to invest the heavy stories vs. a light touch.
- **Lead cast** — which of your 6 characters carry the scene.
- **The hook** — a 1–2 line story seed (the error / pressure that opens the topic).
- **🔗 Linkage** — a past callback or a future setup, so the universe feels connected and the student's *exam memory* threads across chapters.
- **🎨 Format** — my suggestion: **TEXT** (narration in class) vs **ANIM** (worth animating) vs **RECURRING-GAG** (cheap to animate, high recall).

A legend at the bottom collects every linkage thread in one place.

## 🧵 The Spine: One Running Storyline

Instead of 36 disconnected scenes, the universe has **one season-long arc** that the AS topics hang off:

> **ECPL (CFO Sir's startup) is preparing for a big event** — raising a funding round and acquiring a smaller company — while **BCPL (Sethji's old empire)** is being cleaned up for a bank loan. Across the year, Accounting Doctors (Diya + Rolly) keep the books, Pranav + Gurpreet Sir audit both, and *every* AS surfaces naturally as part of getting these two companies "audit-ready."

This gives us **built-in future linkage**: the funding round (Ch 1–3 framework) → asset clean-up (Ch 4–10) → liability & provisioning (Ch 11–13) → profit story (Ch 14–20) → full statements (Ch 21–26) → the acquisition itself (Ch 27–31) → capital surgery (Ch 32–34). The acquisition that's *teased in Chapter 1* finally *happens in Chapter 30*. That payoff is the glue.
