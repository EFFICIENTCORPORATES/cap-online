The government-grant interface (AS 12) is the second most recurring pattern — nearly every attempt has at least one grant-related PPE cost/refund question. The Revaluation + machinery-derecognition embedded pattern is worth flagging separately to a student: it is extremely formulaic and appears almost identically across 7 different papers (same two adjustments — land revaluation to Revaluation Reserve, and removing a disposed machine's cost/depreciation before computing the year's charge) — high-value to master since it recurs so predictably. 2.1, 2.4, and Disclosure requirements have essentially never been tested as their own question in this bank.
