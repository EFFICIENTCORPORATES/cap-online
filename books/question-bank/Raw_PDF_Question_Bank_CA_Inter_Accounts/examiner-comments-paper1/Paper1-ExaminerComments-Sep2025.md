PAPER – 1: ADVANCED ACCOUNTING  
Specific Comments  
Question 1.(a)  A significant number of examinees lacked a proper 
understanding of the provisions of AS 22 “Accounting for Taxes on 
Income”. Many w ere unable to compute the amount of current tax, deferred 
tax asset/liability and total tax expense for each of the years. Incorrect 
identification of timing difference s and inappropriate application of tax 
treatment were frequently observed.  
(b) The performance of examinees in this part was not satisfactory. A majority 
of them were unable to determine the value of closing inventory of finished 
goods (MP) and raw ma terials (RP) in accordance with AS 2 “Valuation of 
Inventories” . Several examinees failed  to compute the cost of production 
correctly, particularly where certain items required proportionate allocation. 
Many could ascertain only the labour cost of `  3,23,200, while the remaining 
elements of cost were incorrectly computed. Further, a large number of 
examinees valued the closing stock of finished goods (on NRV basis) and raw 
materials (on replacement cost basis) incorrectly.  
(c) The responses of examinee s to the requirements of AS 9 “Revenue 
Recognition”  were unsatisfactory. Many were unable to c orrectly comment 
on the treatment of the four independent situations provided in the question, 
indicating inadequate conceptual clarity on timing and basis of revenue 
recognition.  
Question 2 .  Many examinees were unable to classify the items correctly in 
the Balance Sheet as per the format prescribed under Schedule III to the 
Companies Act, 2013 . Errors were frequently noted in computing the balance 
of Reserves & Surplus and in giving effect to the revaluation gain relating to 
PPE. Several examinees also f ailed to show the proper treatment of claims 
against the company amounting to ` 4,15,000 which had not been 
acknowledged as debt. Only a few examinees prepared the N otes to Accounts 
in the prescribed manner.

Question 3 .(a)  Some examinees prepared the Cash Flow Statement from 
Operating Activities correctly. However, many omitted to give effect to the

Sheet and Notes to Accounts in accordance with Schedule III to the 
Companies Act, 2013,  using the information given. Many also failed to 
compute the correct amount of purchase consideration and goodwill. These 
errors had a consequential impact on the preparation of the Balance Sheet.  
Question 5 .  Many examinees were unable to prepare the Bala nce Sheet and

these errors, the computation of capital profits (pre -acquisition) and revenue 
profits (post -acquisition) was incorrect, which further affected the final 
answers.
