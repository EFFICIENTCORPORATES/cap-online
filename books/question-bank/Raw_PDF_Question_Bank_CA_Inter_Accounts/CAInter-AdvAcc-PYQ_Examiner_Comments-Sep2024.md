PAPER – 1: ADVANCED ACCOUNTING  
Specific Comments  
Question 1.(a)  Most of the examinees correctly calculated the amount of 
amortization as per the provisions of AS 26 , However, some failed to pass the 
correct journal entries for amortization in compliance with AS 26.  
(b) Many examinees calculated the actual return on plan assets correctly , 
however, most of the examinees failed to calculate the expected return in line 
with the provisions of AS 15 “Employee Benefits ”. 
(c)  A majority of examinees accurately calculated the deferred tax amount for 
the first year and its reversal in the second and third years. However, they 
incorrectly classified it as a deferred tax asset instead of a deferred tax 
liability. Additionally, many failed to compute the total tax expense in 
compliance with AS 22, Accounting for Taxes on Income.  
Question 2 . Most of the examinees were not able to classify the various items 
in the Balance Sheet and Statement of Profit and Loss as per Schedule III 
format prescribed under the Companies Act, 2013. They also missed adjusting 
for the issue of bonus shares from general reserves and the securities 
premium account.  A few examinees incorrectly showed the transfer to general 
reserves on the face of the Statement of Profit and Loss rather than disclosing 
it under the Notes on Reserves and Surplus.  In the Statement of Profit and 
Loss, many examinees made errors in calculating  the amount of other income 
and depreciation, resulting in an incorrect profit for the year.  
Question 3 .(a)  Several examinees made errors in calculating amounts paid 
to creditors and the cost of machines purchased. Some mistakenly added cash 
outflows and subtracted cash inflows, resulting in incorrect net changes in 
cash and cash equivalents. Consequently, they failed to prepare the correct 
Cash Flow Statement as per AS -3 for the year ended 31st March, 2024 . 
(b) Many examinees failed to calculate the number of shares eligible for 
buyback under the Debt -Equity Ratio Test. Errors were also observed in the 
Resource Test and Shares Outstanding Test, resulting in incorrect calculations 
of the maximum permissible number of shares for buyback.
