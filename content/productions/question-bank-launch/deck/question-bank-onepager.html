Marks: 5 Approx Time: 9 minutes (approx.) Topic 2.6–2.7: Initial measurement of PPE at cost
"Preet Ltd. is installing a new plant… advise on the costs that can be capitalised in accordance with AS 10 (Revised). (5 Marks)"
Answer / Solution: Cost of plant, delivery & handling, site preparation, consultants' fees, and estimated dismantling cost — total ₹17,20,000 capitalised; pre-commercial operating losses of ₹1,60,000 excluded.
Exhibit III

Learn from real mistakes, not guesses

Every answer carries a note on what usually goes wrong — and you always know exactly whose words you're reading.

  • Examiner's Comment (tan) — a real quote from ICAI's own official "Examiner's Comments on the Performance of the Examinees," wherever one was published for that sitting.
  • Author's Note (pink) — the author's own read on the likely pitfall, clearly labelled as guidance, never dressed up as an ICAI verdict.
The colour codeHonest, always labelled
Examiner's Comment: “Candidates frequently failed to distinguish directly attributable costs from general overheads, capitalising expenditure the standard specifically excludes.”
Author's Note: A common error is including the pre-commercial-production operating losses in the capitalised cost, on the mistaken reasoning that they were incurred before the asset was ready — AS 10 excludes them regardless of timing.

If it's tan, ICAI said it. If it's pink, the author is telling you what usually goes wrong.
