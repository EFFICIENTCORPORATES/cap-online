## B. Cross-holding and Section 19

**You were right.** Section 19 of the Companies Act, 2013 is in the study material, in **AS 21, Topic 1.1, p10.4**:

- A subsidiary company is prohibited from holding shares in its holding company.
- No company may, by itself or through nominees, hold shares in its holding company; no holding company may allot or transfer its shares to a subsidiary, and any such allotment or transfer is void.
- **Three exceptions** (a subsidiary may remain a member of its holding company if it holds the shares): (a) as legal representative of a deceased member; (b) as a trustee; (c) it was already a shareholder before it became a subsidiary.
- Voting: only for shares held as legal representative or trustee.

Where "cross holding" appears: **only in the syllabus Note-1 on p10.2** ("the unit… excludes problems involving acquisition of interest in subsidiary at different dates, cross holding, disposal of a subsidiary and foreign subsidiaries"). The word is not defined anywhere in the six chapters (searched for it, "cross-hold", "reciprocal", "mutual holding").

**So my earlier row was wrong in its wording** ("when the subsidiary also holds shares of the parent"). That is exactly what Sec 19 prohibits, so it is not a normal case to illustrate. In the deck, cross-holding will be shown only as: *"a syllabus exclusion; Sec 19 is why a subsidiary cannot ordinarily hold its parent's shares, with three exceptions"*, with no animation of it as a standard situation.

## D. Corrections to earlier statements (from this session's checking)

1. **Your v4/v5 roadmap topic numbers for AS 21 do not match ICAI's own numbering after 1.14.** ICAI's topic **1.14** (*Consolidation adjustments*, pp 10.27 – 10.72) is one big topic containing lettered sections: **A** Revaluation (p10.27), **B** Dividend received from subsidiary (p10.31), **C** Elimination of intra-group transactions (p10.67), **D** Alignment of reporting dates (p10.72). Then **1.15** = Consolidated P&L (p10.73), **1.16** = Consolidated cash flow (p10.76), **1.17** = Uniform accounting policies (p10.77), **1.18** = Preference share capital (p10.83). Your roadmaps call dividends "1.15", intra-group eliminations "1.16" and uniform policies "1.17"; the page ranges are right, the topic numbers are not.
2. **My earlier remark that v4's JCA page range (10.143–10.148) "falls inside JCO" was wrong.** JCO is topic 3.6 (p10.138 – p10.142) and JCA is topic 3.7 (starts p10.143). v4's JCA pages are right. (v4 says JCO is "Topic 3.5"; ICAI's 3.5 is *Forms of joint ventures* on the same page 10.138.)
3. **"Net results of operations"** is in AS 21's definition of minority interest at p10.6 (topic 1.2) and p10.26 (topic 1.12), not only 1.12.
4. **External reconstruction:** the "substantially the same shareholders" wording **is** in the study material (Ch 14, p14.3), so that part of my earlier correction stands. But the same paragraph says external reconstruction is "essentially covered under the category of 'amalgamation in the nature of merger' in AS 14", which my Scene 4 did not say. The flow now includes it.
5. **AS 27 file end-of-file markers** `printed_page="10.30"` on the last two case scenarios are conversion glitches; do not use them.
