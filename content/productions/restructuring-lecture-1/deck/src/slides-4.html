Mentions · the cut-off line decides all of these

Three small rules ride on the same line

Dividend rule

A dividend from pre-acquisition profit is a return of the investment: credit Investment A/c. From post-acquisition profit it is income.

AS 21 topic 1.14 B, p10.31 – 10.33 · AS 13 topic 3.5, p5.89

Time basis

If control is acquired during the year, profit for the year is split pre/post on the basis of time, unless told otherwise.

AS 21 p10.32

Bonus shares

If the subsidiary issues bonus shares, the source of the reserves used (pre or post) decides the effect.

AS 21 Note 5 p10.33 · example p10.49

Same idea behind all three: profit earned before the date was paid for (capital); profit earned after belongs to the group (revenue).

Joint control · AS 27

When control is shared by contract

JCO

Jointly controlled operations

Each partner uses its own assets and books its own share of revenue and expenses. No new entity.

AS 27 topic 3.6, p10.138

JCA

Jointly controlled assets

An asset owned jointly. Each partner books its share of the asset and of the output.

AS 27 topic 3.7, p10.143

JCE

Jointly controlled entity

A separate company under joint control. Each partner combines its % of every line: proportionate consolidation.

AS 27 topic 3.8, p10.149 · consolidation topic 3.9, p10.154

Equity method = one line that grows and shrinks. Proportionate consolidation = your percentage of every line. Full consolidation = every line, then give the outsiders their share.

AS 18: joint control is the contractually agreed sharing of power to govern the financial and operating policies; it does not depend on voting power (p4.84).
