Layer 2 · how we account for it (the exam test)

The form does not decide the method. Five conditions do.

1 · All assets and liabilities of the transferor come over ✓
2 · 90% or more of the transferor's equity shareholders (by face value) become shareholders of the transferee ✓
3 · Consideration is wholly in equity shares (cash only for fractions) ✓
4 · The business is intended to carry on ✓
5 · No adjustment to book values (except to align accounting policies) ✓
All five passAmalgamation in the nature of MERGER
Accounted by the pooling of interests method
Even one failsAmalgamation in the nature of PURCHASE
Accounted by the purchase method
Two traps. An absorption of your own subsidiary at book values can pass all five (a merger). An A + B → C where the new company revalues assets fails condition 5 (a purchase). Feelings (marriage vs swallowing) do not decide; the five conditions do. ICAI notes external reconstruction is essentially a merger (Ch 14 p14.3).
AS 14 topic 2.3 p9.25 · merger conditions topic 2.4 p9.26 · purchase nature topic 2.5 p9.27 · methods topic 2.6 p9.27

Control · Bhim Ltd crosses the line

Say the word control and five things switch on

Bhim Ltd holds 0%
CONTROL off

 

1 · Line by line. "If I control it, I must report it": Arjun's assets and liabilities enter Bhim's statements.
2 · Cost of control. Goodwill or capital reserve appears.
3 · Pre- and post-acquisition. Profits and reserves are split at the acquisition date.
4 · Minority interest, but only if Bhim owns less than 100%.
5 · Intra-group adjustments. Dealings inside the group are cancelled.
That is why the AS 21 chapter feels crowded: it is five ideas arriving together.

Control · what exactly is control?

AS 18's definition of control is wider than AS 21's

AS 21 · consolidated statements

(a) ownership, directly or indirectly, of more than one-half of the voting power; or

(b) control of the composition of the board of directors so as to obtain economic benefits from its activities.

AS 21 topic 1.2 p10.6 · topic 1.6 p10.11

AS 18 · related parties

(a) ownership of more than one-half of the voting power; or

(b) control of the composition of the board; or

(c) a substantial interest in voting power (20% or more) and the power to direct the financial and/or operating policies, by statute or agreement.

ICAI SM, Presentation & Disclosures Based Accounting Standards, topic 4.6, p4.82 – 4.83

Exclusions from consolidation

Control intended to be temporary; or severe long-term restrictions on the subsidiary. (AS 21 topic 1.7, p10.12 – 10.13)

Syllabus limits for numericals

One subsidiary; no interest acquired at different dates, no cross-holding, no disposal, no foreign subsidiary. (AS 21 Note-1, p10.2)

Mentions · the cut-off line decides all of these

Three small rules ride on the same line

Dividend rule

A dividend from pre-acquisition profit is a return of the investment: credit Investment A/c. From post-acquisition profit it is income.

AS 21 topic 1.14 B, p10.31 – 10.33 · AS 13 topic 3.5, p5.89

Time basis

If control is acquired during the year, profit for the year is split pre/post on the basis of time, unless told otherwise.

AS 21 p10.32

Bonus shares

If the subsidiary issues bonus shares, the source of the reserves used (pre or post) decides the effect.

AS 21 Note 5 p10.33 · example p10.49

Same idea behind all three: profit earned before the date was paid for (capital); profit earned after belongs to the group (revenue).

Joint control · AS 27

When control is shared by contract

JCO

Jointly controlled operations

Each partner uses its own assets and books its own share of revenue and expenses. No new entity.

AS 27 topic 3.6, p10.138

JCA

Jointly controlled assets

An asset owned jointly. Each partner books its share of the asset and of the output.

AS 27 topic 3.7, p10.143

JCE

Jointly controlled entity

A separate company under joint control. Each partner combines its % of every line: proportionate consolidation.

AS 27 topic 3.8, p10.149 · consolidation topic 3.9, p10.154

Equity method = one line that grows and shrinks. Proportionate consolidation = your percentage of every line. Full consolidation = every line, then give the outsiders their share.

AS 18: joint control is the contractually agreed sharing of power to govern the financial and operating policies; it does not depend on voting power (p4.84).

Net assets

What the owners own: assets minus liabilities, which is equity.

Net results of operations

The profit the business earns.

Acquisition

Buying a stake in a company that carries on.

More than half the votes, or the board.

What Bhim paid minus its share of net assets: goodwill or capital reserve.

Profit before the date was paid for (capital); after it is revenue.
