Once access to a purchased PDF is granted, that purchase is final and non-refundable — access is instant, and the full content becomes available to you the moment payment is confirmed. Printed books are shipped prepaid and are non-refundable once dispatched, except where a book arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered — contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we'll arrange a replacement.
