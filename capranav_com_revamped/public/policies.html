Privacy Policy

This section explains what information capranav.com collects, why, and how it's kept.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address, and phone number, given when you place an order or log in.
  • Your delivery address, only if you order a printed book.
  • A one-time login code (OTP) sent to your email when you sign in — this expires shortly after it's sent.
  • A record of which purchased PDFs your account has access to.
  • Any message you send through the contact form, along with the name and email you give with it.

What we don't collect

We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details. Payments are handled directly by Razorpay, our payment gateway; capranav.com only receives confirmation that a payment succeeded, not your payment instrument details.

Why we collect it

To create and fulfil your order, confirm payment, grant access to a purchased PDF, deliver a printed book to the right place, log you in without needing a password, and reply to messages you send us.

Where it's stored

In Cloudflare D1, a database run by Cloudflare on the same infrastructure that serves this website. Login codes, session information, order records, and contact messages are all stored there.

Who we share it with

Only Razorpay (to process your payment) and Cloudflare (to host the site, store data, and send emails), and only as needed to provide the service you're using. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Cookies

A single session cookie is set when you log in, used only to keep you signed in — this site does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting font files from Google's servers.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account information or stop contacting you at any time by emailing capranavpratiktulshyan@gmail.com. Records tied to a completed order may need to be kept for a reasonable period for accounting purposes even after such a request.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

PDF (online reading) access

Once access to a purchased PDF is granted, that purchase is final and non-refundable — access is instant, and the full content becomes available to you the moment payment is confirmed. If a payment was deducted but access was never granted, that's a genuine error on our end — contact us and we'll investigate and fix it.

Printed books

Printed books are shipped prepaid and are non-refundable once dispatched, except where a book arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered — contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we'll arrange a replacement.

Course enrolment

Once your enrolment is confirmed and access to classes or materials has been granted, the purchase is final and non-refundable. If you believe you were charged in error — for example, a duplicate payment — contact us and we'll look into it.

Need help with an order? Email capranavpratiktulshyan@gmail.com with your order details (the email you used, what you purchased, and roughly when) — we aim to respond within a few business days.
