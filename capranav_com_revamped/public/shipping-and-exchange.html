What ships

Only the printed-book option involves physical shipping. PDF access and course access are delivered digitally — there's nothing to ship.

Where we ship

Currently within India only. If you're outside India and would like a printed copy, email us and we'll let you know if it can be arranged.

Cost

Shipping is already included in the printed price shown on the site — there's no separate shipping charge added at checkout.

Timeline

Orders are typically dispatched within a few business days of payment confirmation. Delivery time after dispatch depends on your location and the courier used. These are estimates, not guarantees.

Exchange

If a printed book arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos and we'll arrange a replacement — see our Cancellation and Refund policy for full detail.

Tracking & delivery issues

Once your book is dispatched, we'll reach out using the contact details you gave at checkout. If your delivery address was incorrect, or your order hasn't arrived when expected, contact us as soon as possible so we can help.
