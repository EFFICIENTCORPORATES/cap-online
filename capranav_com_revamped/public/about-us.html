Teaching

More than four years of dedicated teaching, thousands of CA students mentored. I teach CA Inter Advanced Accounting through VC Gurukul, Noida, and teach accounting and GST to working professionals through Newton of Accounts.

Consolidation, Ind AS, closing entries and audit checks are not abstract for me: I have done them for large companies. That is why I teach the concept first and then show how it looks in a real ledger, building from basics to the level ICAI examines.

capranav.com is where I sell my CA Inter Gr.1 Advanced Accounting course, my two books (The Question Bank Book and The Exam Strategy Book — each available as a printed copy or PDF access), and point students to free MCQ practice on Telegram.
