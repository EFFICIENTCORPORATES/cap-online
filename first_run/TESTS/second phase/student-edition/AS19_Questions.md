**4.** <p>As per AS 19, unearned finance income is:</p>

- **(A)** the difference between (a) the gross investment in the lease and (b) the present value of
      minimum lease payments under a finance lease from the standpoint of the lessor; and any unguaranteed
      residual value accruing to the lessor, at the interest rate implicit in the lease.
- **(B)** the payments over the lease term that the lessee is, or can be required, to make excluding
      contingent rent, costs for services and taxes to be paid by and reimbursed to the lessor.
- **(C)** the aggregate of the minimum lease payments under a finance lease from the standpoint of
      the lessor and any unguaranteed residual value accruing to the lessor.
