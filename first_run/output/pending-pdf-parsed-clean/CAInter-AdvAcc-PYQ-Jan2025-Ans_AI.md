In case where fair value of plan assets is high, it may so happen that the net amount under defined benefit liability turns negative (giving rise to net assets).

As per AS 15 the enterprise, in such a situation, should measure the resulting asset at the lower of:

(i) the amount so determined, i.e. ₹ 10 lakh; and
