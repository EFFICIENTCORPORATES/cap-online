PYQ May 2026 · Q6(a) [OR-alt 1] · Marks: 4 · Approx Time: 8 minutes (approx.) · M1_C1_U0 : Introduction to Accounting Standards / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 5
Question:

Explain the key aspects which required the need for convergence towards global standards of financial reporting. (4 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

A few key aspects which required the need for convergence are as under:

1. Raising funds from international markets: Each country has its own set of rules and regulations for accounting and financial reporting. Therefore, when an enterprise decides to raise capital from markets other than the country in which it is located, the rules and regulations of that other country will apply, requiring the enterprise to understand the differences between the rules governing financial reporting in the foreign country as compared to its own.

2. Comparability of Financial Statements: International analysts and investors would like to compare financial statements based on similar ASs, leading to a growing need for an internationally accepted set of ASs for cross-border filings, raising investor confidence generally.

3. Uniformity, Comparability, Transparency etc.: A strong need was felt to bring about uniformity, rationalisation, comparability, transparency and adaptability in financial statements. A multiplicity of AS systems worldwide produces different reported numbers for the same events, creating confusion and encouraging error or fraud. The cure is a single set of global standards, of the highest quality, set in the public interest.

4. Global Investment: Convergence contributes to the free flow of global investment, improves investors' ability to compare investments globally (lowering risk of errors of judgment), facilitates accounting/reporting for companies with global operations, eliminates costly requirements like reinstatement of financial statements, creates a new standard of accountability and transparency, and reduces operational challenges for accounting firms.

Author’s Note: Several examinees listed only one or two convergence drivers instead of the full range (fund-raising, comparability, uniformity, global investment), losing marks for an incomplete answer to a theory question that explicitly asks for "key aspects" in the plural.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

RTP May 2026 · Q5 · Marks: Not stated in source · Approx Time: 10–20 minutes (approx.) · M1_C1_U0 : Introduction to Accounting Standards / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 6
Question:

Write a short note on International Accounting Standard Board (IASB).

Answer / Solution:

With a view of achieving the objective of setting global standards, the London based group namely the International Accounting Standards Committee (IASC), responsible for developing International Accounting Standards (IAS), was established in June, 1973.

  • It is presently known as International Accounting Standards Board (IASB).
  • Between 1973 and 2001, the IASC released IASs. Between 1997 and 1999, the IASC restructured their organisation, which resulted in formation of IASB. These changes came into effect on 1st April, 2001. Subsequently, IASB issued statements about current and future standards.
  • IASB publishes its Standards in a series of pronouncements called International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS).
  • The standards issued by IASC till 31.03.2001 are known as IASs and the standards issued by IASB since 01.04.2001 are known as IFRSs.
Author’s Note: Some examinees conflated the IASC and IASB timelines, or reversed which body issued IASs versus IFRSs, resulting in imprecise historical sequencing in their responses.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.
