MTP January 2026 Set 2 · Q1(c) · Marks: 4 · Approx Time: 8 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U6 : AS 24 — Discontinuing Operations / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 6.2 Compulsory
Question:

Kumar Ltd. manufactures both passenger cars and commercial vehicles. Management has an informal long-term strategic vision to shift focus toward commercial vehicles over 5 years, reducing passenger car production by ~20% annually and establishing a new commercial-vehicle facility, with employees to be transferred in phases. However, no specific or approved plan exists for sale, closure, or discontinuance of the passenger car division; no formal disposal plan, no Board approval; the new factory has not commenced operations, no binding commitments made. Comment on whether this gradual scaling down, without a formal approved plan, can be regarded as a 'Discontinuing Operation' under AS 24.

Answer / Solution:

Mere gradual phasing out is not considered a discontinuing operation as defined under AS 24. Examples of activities that do not necessarily satisfy the definition on their own (though they might in combination with other circumstances) include: gradual/evolutionary phasing out of a product line; discontinuing several products within an ongoing line of business; shifting production/marketing activities between locations; and closing a facility for productivity improvements or cost savings.

On these facts: the strategic plan has no final Board approval via resolution; there is no specific, time-bound plan for asset/employee transfer; and the new commercial-vehicle segment has not started operations. Kumar Ltd.'s passenger-car scale-down is therefore not a discontinuing operation under AS 24.

Author’s Note: Some examinees treated the mere existence of a strategic intention as sufficient for AS 24 classification, missing that a specific, Board-approved plan with a defined implementation timeline is required.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

MTP May 2026 Set 1 · Q6(a) [OR-alt 1] · Marks: 5 · Approx Time: 9 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U6 : AS 24 — Discontinuing Operations / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 6.5
Question:

Analyse the disclosure and presentation requirements of AS 24 for Discontinuing Operations (any five). (5 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

An enterprise should include the following information relating to a discontinuing operation in its financial statements beginning with the financial statements for the period in which the initial disclosure event occurs:

  1. A description of the discontinuing operation(s).
  2. The business or geographical segment(s) in which it is reported as per AS 17.
  3. The date and nature of the initial disclosure event.
  4. The date or period in which the discontinuance is expected to be completed if known or determinable.
  5. The carrying amounts, as of the balance sheet date, of the total assets to be disposed of and the total liabilities to be settled.
  6. The amounts of revenue and expenses in respect of the ordinary activities attributable to the discontinuing operation during the current financial reporting period.
  7. The amount of pre-tax profit or loss from ordinary activities attributable to the discontinuing operation during the current financial reporting period, and the income tax expense related thereto.
  8. The amounts of net cash flows attributable to the operating, investing, and financing activities of the discontinuing operation during the current financial reporting period.
Author’s Note: Many examinees omitted the requirement to disclose the amount of net cash flows attributable to the operating, investing and financing activities of the discontinuing operation, listing only the descriptive disclosures under AS 24.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

PYQ January 2026 · Q6(a) [OR-alt 1] · Marks: 4 · Approx Time: 8 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U6 : AS 24 — Discontinuing Operations / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 6.5
Question:

What is the initial disclosure information of AS-24 for discontinuing operations? (4 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

An enterprise should include the following information relating to a discontinuing operation in its financial statements beginning with the financial statements for the period in which the initial disclosure event occurs: (a) A description of the discontinuing operation(s). (b) The business or geographical segment(s) in which it is reported as per AS 17. (c) The date and nature of the initial disclosure event. (d) The date or period in which the discontinuance is expected to be completed if known or determinable. (e) The carrying amounts, as of the balance sheet date, of the total assets to be disposed of and the total liabilities to be settled. (f) The amounts of revenue and expenses in respect of the ordinary activities attributable to the discontinuing operation during the current financial reporting period. (g) The amount of pre-tax profit or loss from ordinary activities attributable to the discontinuing operation during the current financial reporting period, and the income tax expense related thereto. (h) The amounts of net cash flows attributable to the operating, investing, and financing activities of the discontinuing operation during the current financial reporting period.

Examiner’s Comment: Many examinees faced difficulty in stating the initial disclosure requirements for discontinuing operations as per AS 24. Instead of addressing the required disclosures, they described the circumstances under which a business may be discontinued.Real ICAI Examiner’s Comment, quoted from the official document for this sitting.

PYQ May 2026 · Q5(b) · Marks: 10 · Approx Time: 18 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U6 : AS 24 — Discontinuing Operations / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 6.2
Question:

Oasis Limited runs a famous chain of super markets and restaurants. Restaurant division constitutes 35% of total group revenue. The management of the company decides to close the business of restaurants as the company has been experiencing declining profitability due to increased competition. On 1st April, 2025, the management announced its intention to discontinue the restaurant business and concentrate on Super market segment only. The decision was made through strategic review and the management started actively seeking buyers for the restaurant division. During the year 2025-26, the company has sold 5 restaurants out of 12 restaurants so far as part of the sale.

Comment, whether it will be considered as discontinuing operation or not as per the provisions of AS-24? (10 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

As per AS 24, a discontinuing operation is a component of an enterprise: that the enterprise, pursuant to a single plan, is disposing of substantially in its entirety, or disposing of piecemeal, or terminating through abandonment; and which represents a separate major line of business or geographical area of operations.

Hence the restaurant division's closure by Oasis Limited will be considered a discontinuing operation as per the provisions of AS 24. This is due to the fact that the assets are sold off as part of a single plan, and that the business represents a separate major line of business, and can be distinguished both operationally and for financial reporting purposes.

Author’s Note: Some examinees concluded this was not a discontinuing operation because the disposal was piecemeal (5 of 12 restaurants sold rather than the whole division at once), not recognising that AS 24 explicitly includes piecemeal disposal pursuant to a single plan within its definition.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

RTP May 2026 · Q15 · Marks: Not stated in source · Approx Time: 10–20 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U6 : AS 24 — Discontinuing Operations / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 6.2/6.3
Question:

Arzoo Ltd. is in the business of manufacture of Passenger cars and commercial vehicles. The company is working on a strategic plan to shift from the Passenger car segment over the coming 5 years. However, no specific plans have been drawn up for sale of neither the division nor its assets. As part of its plan it will reduce the production of passenger cars by 20% annually. It also plans to commence another new factory for the manufacture of commercial vehicles plus transfer of employees in a phased manner.

(i) You are required to comment if mere gradual phasing out in itself can be considered as a ‘Discontinuing Operation' within the meaning of AS 24.

(ii) If the company passes a resolution to sell some of the assets in the passenger car division and also to transfer few other assets of the passenger car division to the new factory, does this trigger the application of AS 24?

(iii) Would your answer to the above be different if the company resolves to sell the assets of the Passenger Car Division in a phased but time bound manner?

Answer / Solution:

Mere gradual phasing out is not considered as discontinuing operation as defined under para 3 of AS 24, ‘Discontinuing Operations'.

Examples of activities that do not necessarily satisfy criterion of the definition, but that might do so in combination with other circumstances, include: (1) Gradual or evolutionary phasing out of a product line or class of service; (2) Discontinuing, even if relatively abruptly, several products within an ongoing line of business; (3) Shifting of some production or marketing activities for a particular line of business from one location to another; and (4) Closing of a facility to achieve productivity improvements or other cost savings.

In view of the above the answers are:

(i) No, the companies' strategic plan has no final approval from the board through a resolution and there is no specific time bound activities like shifting of assets and employees. Above all, the new segment i.e. commercial vehicle production line in a new factory has not started.

(ii) No, the resolution is salient about stoppage of the Car segment in definite time period. Though, sale of some assets and some transfer proposal were passed through a resolution to the new factory, closure road map and new segment starting roadmap are missing. Hence, AS 24 will not be applicable.

(iii) Yes, phased and time bound programme resolved in the board clearly indicates the closure of the passenger car segment in a definite time frame and will constitute a clear roadmap. Hence, this action will attract compliance of AS 24.

Author’s Note: Some examinees answered part (iii) without properly building on the reasoning already established in parts (i) and (ii), missing that a phased-but-time-bound board resolution is precisely the missing ingredient (a definite closure roadmap) that parts (i) and (ii) found absent, and so overlooked the requirement for a clear sale plan with time-bound actions when applying AS 24.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.
