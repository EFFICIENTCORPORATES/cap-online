PYQ September 2025 · Q4 · Marks: 14 · Approx Time: 26 minutes (approx.) · M3_C13_U0 : Amalgamation of Companies / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 2
Question:

Blue Limited and Yellow Limited Balance Sheets as at 31st March 2025 (₹ lakhs): Blue — Equity Shares of ₹100 40.000, 10% Debentures 15.000, General Reserves 1.500, Retirement Gratuity Fund 3.450, Trade Payables 7.400, Other current liabilities 1.240, Short-term provisions 0.710; Goodwill 8.750, PPE 35.650, Inventories 8.890, Trade Receivables 13.535, Cash in hand 0.485, Bank 1.990. Yellow — Equity Shares of ₹100 28.000, 8% Debentures 8.000, General Reserves 0.670, Retirement Gratuity Fund 1.300, Trade Payables 4.250, Other current liabilities 0.880, Short-term provisions 0.320; Goodwill 1.795, PPE 31.260, Inventories 4.800, Trade Receivables 4.650, Cash in hand 0.315, Bank 0.600. Blue absorbs Yellow's business on 31st March 2025: (i) 8% Debenture holders of Yellow paid at 10% discount via 10% Debentures at par in Blue. (ii) Unrecorded current asset ₹1,16,000 in Yellow's books, taken over. (iii) Yellow's Trade Payables include ₹1,50,000 payable to Blue. (iv) Yellow's Inventory taken over at 10% more than book value. (v) Yellow's Goodwill = 2 × simple average profits of 2021-22 (4,50,000), 2022-23 (3,90,000), 2023-24 (2,35,000); 2022-23 had a ₹50,000 cash embezzlement already adjusted in that year's profit. (vi) Yellow's PPE taken over at ₹36,44,500. (vii) Remaining assets/liabilities at book value. (viii) Yellow's equity shareholders issued Blue shares at 5% premium. Calculate the Purchase Consideration and prepare Blue's post-absorption Balance Sheet.

Answer / Solution:

W.N.1 Goodwill of Yellow Ltd.: Adjusted profits: 2021-22: 4.50; 2022-23: 3.90+0.50 (add back embezzlement, a one-off/non-recurring item) = 4.40; 2023-24: 2.35. Total 11.25; average (i)/3 = 3.75; ×2 years' purchase = Goodwill ₹7.50 lakhs.

(i) Purchase Consideration: Goodwill 7.500 + PPE 36.445 + Inventory (4.800×1.10=5.280) + Trade receivables 4.650 + Cash in hand 0.315 + Cash at Bank 0.600 + Unrecorded assets 1.160 = Assets taken over 55.950; less liabilities taken over (10% Debentures at 90% of 8.00=7.200 + Retirement Gratuity 1.300 + Trade payables 4.250 + Other current liabilities 0.880 + Short-term provisions 0.320 = 13.950); Net Assets/Purchase Consideration = ₹42.000 lakhs, satisfied by issuing 40,000 (42 lakhs/₹105) equity shares of Blue Ltd. at 5% premium.

Balance Sheet of Blue Ltd. (after absorption) as at 31.3.2025: Share Capital 80.000 (40,000 original + 40,000 new shares of ₹100); Reserves & Surplus 3.500 (Securities Premium 2.000 + General Reserve 1.500); Long-term Borrowings 22.200 (10% Debentures 15.000+7.200); Long-term Provisions 4.750 (Retirement Gratuity 3.450+1.300); Trade Payables 10.150 (7.400+4.250−1.500 inter-company elimination); Other current liabilities 2.120 (1.240+0.880); Short-term provisions 1.030 (0.710+0.320). Total 123.750. Assets: PPE 72.095 (35.650+36.445); Intangible assets (Goodwill) 16.250 (8.750+7.500); Inventories 14.170 (8.890+5.280); Trade receivables 16.685 (13.535+4.650−1.500); Cash and cash equivalents 3.390 (Cash in hand 0.800 + Bank 2.590); Other current assets 1.160 (unrecorded asset). Total 123.750.

RTP September 2025 · Q15 · Marks: Not stated in source · Approx Time: 10–20 minutes (approx.) · M3_C13_U0 : AS 14 — Amalgamation of Companies / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1
Question:

X Co. Ltd. (share capital ₹50 lakhs, equity shares of ₹10 each) was taken over by Y Co. Ltd. X Co. Ltd. has General Reserve ₹10,00,000 and Profit and Loss account Cr. ₹5,00,000. Y Co. Ltd. issued 11 equity shares of ₹10 each for every 10 shares of X Co. Ltd. Pass the journal entries in Y Co. Ltd.'s books for the shares issued under the Pooling of Interests method.

Answer / Solution:

Purchase consideration = 50,00,000 × 11/10 = ₹55,00,000.

Journal entries (books of Y Co. Ltd.):

  1. Business Purchase A/c Dr. 55,00,000; To Liquidator of X Co. Ltd. 55,00,000 (business of X Co. Ltd. purchased).
  2. Assets A/c (balancing figure) Dr. 65,00,000; To Business Purchase A/c 55,00,000; To General Reserve A/c 5,00,000; To Profit and Loss A/c 5,00,000 (assets and reserves/surplus taken over — under Pooling of Interests, X's reserves are carried forward at their existing book figures, not merged into goodwill).
  3. Liquidator of X Co. Ltd. Dr. 55,00,000; To Equity Share Capital A/c 55,00,000 (purchase consideration discharged via equity shares of Y Co. Ltd.).

W.N.: Purchase consideration ₹55,00,000 − Share capital of X Co. Ltd. ₹50,00,000 = ₹5,00,000 to be adjusted from (i.e., absorbed into) General Reserve.

Author’s Note: Some examinees computed goodwill for the excess of purchase consideration over share capital, applying the Purchase Method's logic instead of the Pooling of Interests method's requirement to adjust the difference directly against reserves.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.
