MTP January 2026 Set 1 · Q6(b) · Marks: 4 · Approx Time: 8 minutes (approx.) · M3_C11_U1 : Preparation of Financial Statements (of Companies) / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.6
Question:

Z Limited as on 31st March, 2024: Equity shares of ₹100 each ₹500 lakhs; General Reserve ₹100 lakhs; Loss for the year ₹5 lakhs. Due to absence of profits, management recommends a 10% dividend on equity share capital out of general reserve. Rates of equity dividend for the last 5 years preceding 2023-24: 2022-23 12%, 2021-22 14%, 2020-21 10%, 2019-20 10%, 2018-19 7%. As an accountant, suggest whether the recommendation is justified; if not, suggest the rate of dividend.

Answer / Solution:

Three conditions govern declaring dividend out of free reserves (Companies (Declaration and Payment of Dividend) Rules): (1) Rate ≤ average rate of last 3 years: 10% < 12% [(12+14+10)/3] — satisfied. (2) Amount drawn ≤ 10% of (paid-up capital + free reserves): 55,00,000 drawn (10% dividend 50,00,000 + loss 5,00,000) vs. maximum 60,00,000 [10% of (500+100) lakhs] — satisfied. (3) Reserves after withdrawal ≥ 15% of paid-up capital: balance after withdrawal (100−55=45 lakhs) is less than 75 lakhs (15% of 500 lakhs) — not satisfied.

Since condition (3) fails, the 10% recommendation is not justified. Maximum permissible withdrawal = opening reserves (100 lakhs) − minimum reserves to retain (75 lakhs, i.e. 15% of paid-up capital) = 25 lakhs; less current year's loss (5 lakhs) = 20 lakhs maximum divisible profit. Permissible dividend rate = 20,00,000 / 5,00,00,000 = 4%.

Author’s Note: Some examinees checked only the rate-cap and amount-cap conditions, missing the third condition (post-withdrawal reserves must remain at least 15% of paid-up capital), which is the one that actually fails here.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

PYQ January 2026 · Q6(b) · Marks: 4 · Approx Time: 8 minutes (approx.) · M3_C11_U1 : Companies (Declaration and Payment of Dividend) Rules 2014 — Preparation of Financial Statements (of Companies) / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.6
Question:

Figures from the financial statements of MC Limited for the year ended 31st March, 2025: Issued, Subscribed and Paid-up Share Capital – 8,00,000 Equity Shares of ₹5 each ₹40,00,000; 1,25,000, 8% Preference Shares of ₹10 each ₹12,50,000. Reserves and Surplus – Securities Premium Reserve ₹5,00,000; Capital Reserve ₹3,00,000; Capital Redemption Reserve ₹2,50,000; General Reserve ₹11,75,000. Net Profit for 2024-25 ₹2,25,000. Equity Dividend paid: 2019-20 10%, 2020-21 11%, 2021-22 12%, 2022-23 13%, 2023-24 14%. Constrained by inadequate profits in 2024-25, management proposes a dividend of 12.5% out of reserves.

You are required to ascertain the amount that can be utilized by the company out of its reserves in accordance with the Companies (Declaration of dividend out of Reserves) Rules, 2014. (4 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

Three conditions apply under the Rules: (1) Rate declared must not exceed the average of rates declared in the three years immediately preceding (2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24: average = (12%+13%+14%)/3 = 13%); the proposed 12.5% is lower, satisfying condition 1. (2) Maximum amount drawn from accumulated profits and reserves must not exceed 10% of paid-up capital and free reserves = 10% of (40,00,000+12,50,000+11,75,000) = ₹6,42,500 (as printed) – alternatively computed as 10% of ₹66,50,000 (including current year profit in free reserves) = ₹6,65,000. (3) Balance of reserves after drawal must not fall below 15% of paid-up capital = 15% of ₹52,50,000 = ₹7,87,500.

12.5% Dividend on ₹40,00,000 equity capital = ₹5,00,000. Current year profit ₹2,25,000 less 8% preference dividend on ₹12,50,000 (₹1,00,000) leaves ₹1,25,000 available from current profit; the remaining ₹3,75,000 must come from accumulated reserves. Since all three conditions are satisfied and the required ₹5,00,000 (or, on the alternative computation, ₹3,75,000 needed against a maximum usable ₹6,12,500 after maintaining the 15% minimum reserve balance) is within the permitted limits, the company can utilize ₹5,00,000 in total (₹1,25,000 from current year profit and ₹3,75,000 from accumulated reserves) as per the Companies (Declaration of Dividend out of Reserves) Rules, 2014.

Examiner’s Comment: Many examinees were unfamiliar with the three conditions for declaring dividends from accumulated profits. They failed to compute the dividend amount correctly. While some examinees were able to apply the provisions relating to declaration of dividend under the Companies (Declaration of Dividend out of Reserves) Rules, 2014, several committed errors in determining the distributable amount and related adjustments. Although many could correctly calculate the average rate of dividend for the preceding three years and the dividend on preference shares, they failed to correctly apply the limits of 10% and 15%.Real ICAI Examiner’s Comment, quoted from the official document for this sitting.
