MTP January 2026 Set 1 · Q3 · Marks: 14 · Approx Time: 26 minutes (approx.) · M2_C10_U1 : AS 21 — Consolidated Financial Statements / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.9
Question:

GB Limited acquired 80% of equity shares of TB Limited on 1st April, 2018 at a cost of ₹58,00,000, when TB Limited had Equity Share Capital of ₹50,00,000 and Reserves and Surplus of ₹4,64,000. Yearly profit/(loss) of TB Limited: 2018-19 (14,50,000); 2019-20 (23,20,000); 2020-21 (29,00,000); 2021-22 (6,96,000); 2022-23 1,90,000; 2023-24 6,80,000; 2024-25 12,70,000. You are required to calculate the minority interest and cost of control at the end of each year for the purpose of consolidation.

Answer / Solution:

W.N. — Minority Interest and Goodwill on 1.4.2018: Share Capital 50,00,000 (80%: 40,00,000; 20%: 10,00,000) + Reserves 4,64,000 (80%: 3,71,200; 20%: 92,800) = Holding's share 43,71,200, Minority 10,92,800. Cost of investment 58,00,000 − 43,71,200 = Goodwill 14,28,800.

Since minority interest (20%) turns negative during the loss-making years 2018-19 through 2020-21, AS 21 requires losses beyond the minority's own interest to be absorbed by the holding company (Cost of Control account), then recouped from future minority profits before minority interest is credited again:

YearProfit/(Loss)Minority share (20%)Minority interest balanceCost of Control (holding co. absorption)
2018-19(14,50,000)(2,90,000)8,02,800—
2019-20(23,20,000)(4,64,000)3,38,800—
2020-21(29,00,000)(5,80,000)Nil (excess 2,41,200 absorbed by holding co.)2,41,200
2021-22(6,96,000)(1,39,200)Nil (absorbed by holding co.)3,80,400 (cumulative)
2022-231,90,00038,000Nil (profit share adjusted against losses absorbed by holding co.)3,42,400 (cumulative)
2023-246,80,0001,36,000Nil (profit share adjusted against holding co.'s absorbed balance)2,06,400 (cumulative)
2024-2512,70,0002,54,00047,600 (2,54,000 − 2,06,400 remaining absorbed balance recouped)Nil

Cumulative Minority Interest at 31.3.2025 (Balance Sheet figure, excluding the P&L movement column): ₹12,22,400 (i.e. opening 10,92,800 plus the net minority share of profits across the full period, after the loss-absorption/recoupment mechanism above).

Author’s Note: A very common error across multiple sittings on this exact pattern: continuing to show a negative (debit) minority interest balance in the consolidated balance sheet, instead of transferring the excess loss to the holding company (Cost of Control) and recouping it against the minority's later profit shares before crediting minority interest again.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

Question:

Seva Limited and subsidiary Meva Limited, Balance Sheets as at 31st March 2025 (₹ Lakh): Seva — Equity Shares of ₹10 6,000, General Reserve 1,500, P&L 2,000, 10% Debentures 1,180, Trade Payable 1,500; Land and Building 4,000, Plant & Machinery 1,460, Investments (Meva shares) 3,000, Inventories 1,400, Trade Receivable 1,420, Cash at Bank 550, Cash in Hand 350. Meva — Equity Shares of ₹10 4,000, General Reserve 1,200, P&L 1,500, 10% Debentures 820, Trade Payable 1,080; Land and Building 4,150, Plant & Machinery 1,650, Inventories 1,100, Trade Receivable 1,000, Cash at Bank 600, Cash in Hand 100. Seva acquired 80% of Meva on 1st April 2024 (Meva's General Reserve ₹2,000 lakh, P&L ₹800 lakh on that date). On 1st November 2024, Meva issued a 1:3 bonus share out of General Reserve (as at 31 March 2024 balance). On 31st March 2025, Seva's inventory includes goods purchased from Meva for ₹125 lakh, on which Meva made a profit of ₹25 lakh. Prepare the Consolidated Balance Sheet as per Schedule III.

Answer / Solution:

W.N.9 Bonus shares: Meva's shares post-bonus 4,000 lakh (at ₹10 face value, i.e. 400 lakh shares) represent 4/3 of pre-bonus holding; pre-bonus = 4,000×3/4=3,000; bonus issued = 4,000−3,000=1,000 (lakh, i.e. ₹1,000 lakh face value), funded from General Reserve.

W.N.1 Meva's General Reserve movement: Opening 2,000 − Bonus 1,000 + transfer from P&L 200 = Closing 1,200.

W.N.2 Meva's P&L movement: Opening 800 − transfer to General Reserve 200 + Net profit for the year (balancing) 900 = Closing 1,500.
