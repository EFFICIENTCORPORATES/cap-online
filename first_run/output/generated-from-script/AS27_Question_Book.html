MTP January 2026 Set 2 · Q3 · Marks: 14 · Approx Time: 26 minutes (approx.) · M2_C10_U3 : AS 27 — Financial Reporting of Interests in Joint Ventures / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.4
Question:

Hari Ltd., OM Ltd. and Bhakti Ltd. jointly constructed a pipeline to transport gas manufactured by them (a jointly controlled asset under AS 27). Expenditure incurred: Buildings ₹18,00,000 (depreciate @5% p.a.); Pipeline ₹90,00,000 (@15% p.a.); Computers & electronics ₹4,50,000 (@40% p.a.); Vehicles ₹13,50,000 (@20% p.a.). They equally bear pipeline maintenance expenditure of ₹9,00,000 each year. Show the extract of the consolidated balance sheet and the extract of Statement of Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet for each venturer.

Answer / Solution:

Each venturer records its own one-third share of each jointly-controlled asset directly in its own books, per AS 27's proportionate-consolidation treatment for a jointly controlled asset (no separate joint entity exists).

Per-venturer figures (identical for each of Hari/OM/Bhakti, one-third share each): Land & Building 6,00,000, less depreciation @5% (30,000) = 5,70,000. Plant & Machinery (Pipeline) 30,00,000, less depreciation @15% (4,50,000) = 25,50,000. Computers 1,50,000, less depreciation @40% (60,000) = 90,000. Vehicles 4,50,000, less depreciation @20% (90,000) = 3,60,000. Total PPE per venturer: ₹35,70,000. Depreciation and amortisation expense per venturer: 30,000+4,50,000+60,000+90,000 = ₹6,30,000. Other operating expenses (pipeline maintenance, one-third share) ₹3,00,000.

Consolidated (all three venturers combined) extract: PPE — Land & Building 3×5,70,000=17,10,000; Plant & Machinery 3×25,50,000=76,50,000; Computers 3×90,000=2,70,000; Vehicles 3×3,60,000=10,80,000; Total Consolidated PPE: ₹1,07,10,000.

Author’s Note: Some examinees treated this as a jointly controlled entity requiring proportionate consolidation of a separate set of books, rather than recognising that a jointly controlled asset (no separate entity) means each venturer simply records its own share directly in its own books.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

RTP January 2026 · Q18 · Marks: Not stated in source · Approx Time: 10–20 minutes (approx.) · M2_C10_U3 : AS 27 — Financial Reporting of Interests in Joint Ventures / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.2
Question:

Briefly explain the scope and forms of Joint Venture as per AS 27.

Answer / Solution:

Scope: AS 27 applies to accounting for interests in joint ventures and reporting joint venture assets, liabilities, income, and expenses in the financial statements of venturers and investors, regardless of the structure/form under which the joint venture activities take place. Its consolidated-financial-statements provisions apply only when the venturer prepares and presents CFS.

Forms of Joint Venture: (i) Jointly controlled operations — no separate entity created; venturers use their own resources, raise their own funds, incur expenses, and realise sales individually, using shared assets/employees alongside their own business, with no separate books maintained for the joint venture. (ii) Jointly controlled assets — no separate legal entity, but the venturers jointly own assets used to generate economic benefit for each of them, taking up related expenses/liabilities per the contractual agreement. (iii) Jointly controlled entities — a new entity is created, pooling the venturers' resources; the entity purchases its own assets, incurs its own liabilities/expenses, and makes its own sales; the net result is shared per the contractual agreement, which also determines joint control; a separate set of books is maintained.

Author’s Note: Some examinees conflated jointly controlled assets with jointly controlled entities, missing that the defining distinction is the absence vs. presence of a separate legal entity and separate books.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.
