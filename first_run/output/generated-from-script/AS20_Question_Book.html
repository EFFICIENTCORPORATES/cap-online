MTP January 2026 Set 1 · Q6(a) [OR-alt 2] · Marks: 4 · Approx Time: 8 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U5 : AS 20 — Earnings Per Share / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 5.6
Question:

On 1st April, 2025, ABC Limited: 50,000 equity shares of ₹100 each (₹80 paid up by all shareholders) ₹40,00,000; 2,00,000 8% Preference shares of ₹10 each ₹20,00,000; 10,000 12% Debentures of ₹100 each (each convertible into 3 equity shares of ₹100 each) ₹10,00,000. On 1st July 2025, remaining ₹20 called up and paid by all shareholders except one holding 10,000 equity shares. Profit after tax for 2025-26: ₹3,44,000. Tax rate 30%. Compute Basic and Diluted EPS.

Answer / Solution:

Basic EPS: Net profit attributable to equity = 3,44,000 − preference dividend (8% × 20,00,000 = 1,60,000) = 1,84,000. Weighted average equity shares: 1 Apr–30 Jun (50,000 shares × 80/100 paid × 3/12) = 10,000 + 1 Jul–31 Mar (40,000 fully-paid shares × 9/12) = 30,000 + (10,000 shares still ₹80-paid × 80/100 × 9/12) = 6,000; total = 46,000. Basic EPS = 1,84,000 / 46,000 = ₹4 per share.

Diluted EPS: Interest on 10,000 12% debentures = 1,20,000; tax relief @30% = 36,000; net interest add-back = 84,000. Adjusted net profit = 1,84,000 + 1,20,000 − 36,000 = 2,68,000. Shares from conversion (10,000 debentures × 3) = 30,000. Diluted shares = 46,000 + 30,000 = 76,000. Diluted EPS = 2,68,000 / 76,000 = ₹3.53 per share (approx).

Author’s Note: Some examinees forgot to add back the after-tax interest saving on the convertible debentures to net profit when computing diluted EPS, or omitted weighting the partly-paid shares by the proportion actually paid up.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

MTP May 2026 Set 2 · Q6(b) · Marks: 5 · Approx Time: 9 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U5 : AS 20 — Earnings Per Share / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 5.4
Question:

In the following list of shares issued, for the purpose of calculation of weighted average number of shares, from which date, weight is to be considered: (i) Equity Shares issued in exchange of cash, (ii) Equity Shares issued as a result of conversion of a debt instrument, (iii) Equity Shares issued in exchange for the settlement of a liability of the enterprise, (iv) Equity Shares issued for rendering of services to the enterprise, (v) Equity Shares issued in lieu of interest and/or principal of an other financial instrument, (vi) Equity Shares issued as consideration for the acquisition of an asset other than in cash. (5 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

(i) Date of Cash receivable. (ii) Date of conversion. (iii) Date on which settlement becomes effective. (iv) When the services are rendered. (v) Date when interest ceases to accrue. (vi) Date on which the acquisition is recognised.

Author’s Note: Many examinees used the date of the board resolution or agreement for several of these cases instead of the specific triggering event AS 20 identifies (e.g. date of conversion, date services are rendered), inflating or understating the weighted average share count.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

PYQ January 2026 · Q1(c) · Marks: 5 · Approx Time: 9 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U5 : AS 20 — Earnings Per Share / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 5.5 Compulsory
Question:

From the financial records of P Limited: Profit before tax FY2023-24 ₹25,00,000; FY2024-25 ₹32,50,000. Number of Equity Shares (Face Value ₹5) outstanding as on 31st March, 2024: 12,00,000. Applicable Tax Rate for both years: 20%. On 1st June, 2024, Directors announced a rights issue: 1 share for every 6 held @ ₹20/share, exercisable to 30th June, 2024, fully subscribed. Fair value of one equity share immediately prior to exercise of right (30th June, 2024) was ₹25.

You are required to calculate the Basic Earnings per Share to be reported in the financial statements of P Limited for the year ended 31st March, 2025, including the comparative figure for the year ended 31st March, 2024, in accordance with AS 20. (5 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

Computation of Basic Earnings Per Share (as per paragraphs 10 and 26 of AS 20):

Working Note 1 – Net Profit after tax (NPAT): 2024 = 25,00,000 − 20% tax = ₹20,00,000. 2025 = 32,50,000 − 20% tax = ₹26,00,000.

Working Note 2 – Theoretical ex-rights fair value per share: (Fair value of all outstanding shares immediately prior to rights + Total received from exercise) / (Shares outstanding prior + Shares issued in exercise) = (25×12,00,000 + 20×2,00,000) / (12,00,000+2,00,000) = 3,40,00,000/14,00,000 = ₹24.29.

Working Note 3 – Adjustment factor: Fair value per share prior to exercise / Theoretical ex-rights value = 25/24.29 = 1.029 (approx.).

ParticularsYear 2024 (₹)Year 2025 (₹)
EPS for the year 2024 as originally reported (20,00,000/12,00,000 shares)1.67
EPS for the year 2024 restated for rights issue [20,00,000/(12,00,000×1.029)] = 20,00,000/12,35,2801.62 (approx.)
EPS for the year 2025 including effects of rights issue: 26,00,000 / [(12,00,000×1.029×3/12)+(14,00,000×9/12)] = 26,00,000/13,58,700 shares1.91 (approx.)
Examiner’s Comment: Many examinees were unable to compute Earnings Per Share correctly in accordance with AS 20 "Earnings Per Share." A common error was the use of profit before tax instead of profit after tax for EPS calculation. Additionally, some examinees failed to determine the correct number of equity shares for computation. Mistakes were also observed in calculating profit after tax, the weighted average number of shares, and the bonus element in rights issues.Real ICAI Examiner’s Comment, quoted from the official document for this sitting.

RTP January 2026 · Q16 · Marks: Not stated in source · Approx Time: 10–20 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U5 : AS 20 — Earnings Per Share / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 5.2
Question:

Should appropriation to mandatory reserves be excluded from net profit attributable to equity shareholders? AB Limited must, per an agreement with debenture holders, appropriate profits each year to a specific reserve such that, at redemption, the reserve equals at least half the debenture value. Since these appropriations aren't available for distribution to equity shareholders, AB Limited excluded them from the Basic EPS numerator. Is this correct?

Answer / Solution:

The appropriation to such a mandatory reserve should be included in net profit attributable to equity shareholders for computing Basic EPS — AB Limited's treatment is not correct.

AS 20 para 11 defines the numerator as net profit/loss for the period "attributable to equity shareholders" — after deducting preference dividends and attributable tax, but nothing else. Emphasis on "attributable to equity shareholders" (not "available for distribution") means amounts appropriated to mandatory reserves, though not distributable as dividend, remain attributable to equity shareholders in substance (the reserve still belongs to them, merely earmarked). They should therefore be included in the Basic EPS computation.

Author’s Note: Some examinees conflated "attributable to equity shareholders" with "distributable to equity shareholders," incorrectly excluding non-distributable mandatory-reserve appropriations from the EPS numerator.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.
