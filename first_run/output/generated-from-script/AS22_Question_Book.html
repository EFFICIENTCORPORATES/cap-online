PYQ September 2025 · Q1(a) · Marks: 5 · Approx Time: 9 minutes (approx.) · M2_C7_U4 : AS 22 — Accounting for Taxes on Income / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 4.5 Compulsory
Question:

Amber Limited purchases a building for ₹20,00,000 on 1st April 2021, useful life 4 years, nil scrap value. Tax depreciation: 50% in Year 1, remaining balance in Year 2. Accounting: straight-line method. Profit before depreciation and taxes: 2021-22 ₹18,00,000; 2022-23 ₹22,00,000; 2023-24 ₹25,00,000; 2024-25 ₹30,00,000. Corporate tax rate 30% all years. Calculate Current Tax, Deferred Tax Assets/Liability, and Tax Expense for each year.
