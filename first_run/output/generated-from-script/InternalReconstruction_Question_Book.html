MTP January 2026 Set 1 · Q4(b) · Marks: 8 · Approx Time: 15 minutes (approx.) · M3_C14_U0 : Internal Reconstruction / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 2
Question:

Summarized Balance Sheet of Vijay Ltd. as on 31st March, 2025: Equity Shares of ₹10 each 3,50,000; 8% Cumulative Preference Shares of ₹100 each 1,75,000; 6% Debentures of ₹100 each 1,40,000; Sundry Creditors 1,75,000; Provision for taxation 3,500; Total 8,43,500. Assets: PPE 4,37,500; Investments (Market value ₹33,250) 35,000; Current Assets 3,50,000; P&L A/c (Dr.) 21,000; Total 8,43,500. Scheme of Internal Reconstruction approved and effective 31st March, 2025: (1) Investments brought to market value; (2) Taxation liability settled at ₹5,250 out of Current Assets; (3) balance of P&L written off; (4) equity shares reduced to ₹4 each; (5) preference shares reduced to ₹60 each; (6) debenture rate increased to 9%, debenture holders surrender ₹100 debentures and exchange 1-for-1 for fresh ₹80 debentures; (7) balance Current Assets revalued at ₹1,57,500; (8) PPE written down to 80%; (9) one creditor of ₹70,000 gives up 50% of claim, allotted 8,750 equity shares of ₹4 each in settlement. Pass Journal entries.

Answer / Solution:

Journal entries (books of Vijay Ltd.):

  1. Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 1,750; To Investments A/c 1,750 (investments revalued to market value).
  2. (a) Provision for taxation A/c Dr. 3,500; Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 1,750; To Liability for taxation 5,250 (tax liability ascertained). (b) Liability for Taxation Dr. 5,250; To Current Assets (Bank) 5,250 (tax paid).
  3. Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 21,000; To Profit & Loss A/c 21,000 (P&L debit balance written off).
  4. Equity Share Capital (₹10) A/c Dr. 3,50,000; To Equity Share Capital (₹4) A/c 1,40,000; To Capital Reduction A/c 2,10,000 (conversion of equity capital).
  5. 8% Cumulative Preference Share Capital A/c Dr. 1,75,000; To 8% Cumulative Preference Share Capital A/c 1,05,000; To Capital Reduction A/c 70,000 (conversion of preference capital to ₹60 each).
  6. 6% Debentures A/c Dr. 1,40,000; To 9% Debentures A/c 1,12,000; To Capital Reduction A/c 28,000 (fresh ₹80 debentures issued to existing holders).
  7. Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 1,87,250; To Current Assets A/c 1,87,250 (current assets revalued).
  8. Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 87,500; To Property, Plant & Equipment A/c 87,500 (PPE written down to 80%).
  9. Sundry Creditors A/c Dr. 70,000; To Equity Share Capital (₹4) A/c 35,000; To Capital Reduction A/c 35,000 (creditor's claim settled 50% cash-equivalent-in-shares, 50% given up).
  10. Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 43,750; To Capital Reserve A/c (balancing figure) 43,750 (balance of Capital Reduction transferred to Capital Reserve).
Author’s Note: Some examinees missed crediting Capital Reduction for the "gain" side of each conversion entry (e.g. the difference between old and new share/debenture face values), recording only the reduction without recognising the corresponding Capital Reduction credit.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

MTP January 2026 Set 2 · Q4 · Marks: 14 · Approx Time: 26 minutes (approx.) · M3_C14_U0 : Internal Reconstruction / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 3
Question:

Summarized Balance Sheet of Star Ltd. as on 31st March, 2025: 15,000 8% Preference shares of ₹50 each 7,50,000; 18,750 Equity shares of ₹50 each 9,37,500; Profit and Loss A/c (Dr.) (5,63,750); Loan 7,16,250; Trade Payables 2,58,750; Other Liabilities 43,750; Total 21,42,500. Assets: Building at cost less depreciation 5,00,000; Plant at cost less depreciation 3,35,000; Trademarks and goodwill at cost 3,97,500; Inventory 5,00,000; Trade Receivables 4,10,000; Total 21,42,500. (Preference dividend in arrears 5 years.) Scheme of reconstruction: (i) equity shares reduced ₹50→₹5, shareholders subscribe 3 new equity shares of ₹5 for each held; (ii) preference shareholders forego dividend arrears, accept 4 new 6% preference shares of ₹10 plus 3 new equity shares of ₹5 for each ₹50 preference share; (iii) lenders convert ₹1,87,500 of loan into 15,000 new preference shares of ₹10 plus 7,500 new equity shares of ₹5; (iv) directors subscribe cash for 25,000 new equity shares of ₹5 (in addition to (i)); (v) ₹2,50,000 of cash received used to reduce the loan; (vi) equity capital cancelled applied to write off the P&L debit balance and ₹43,750 from plant, balance to write down trademarks/goodwill; nominal capital increased to ₹8,12,500 (preference) and ₹9,37,500 (equity). Pass journal entries and prepare the post-reconstruction Balance Sheet.

Answer / Solution:

Journal entries (books of Star Ltd.):

  1. Equity Share Capital A/c (₹50) Dr. 9,37,500; To Equity Share Capital A/c (₹5) 93,750; To Capital Reduction A/c 8,43,750 (equity capital reduced to ₹5 nominal).
  2. Bank A/c Dr. 2,81,250; To Equity Share Capital A/c 2,81,250 (3 rights shares per share held, subscribed).
  3. 8% Preference Share Capital A/c (₹50) Dr. 7,50,000; Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 75,000; To 6% Preference Share Capital A/c (₹10) 6,00,000; To Equity Share Capital A/c (₹50) 2,25,000 (conversion, plus 3 equity shares per preference share held).
  4. Loan A/c Dr. 1,87,500; To 6% Preference Share Capital A/c 1,50,000 (15,000 × ₹10); To Equity Share Capital A/c 37,500 (7,500 × ₹5) (loan converted to shares).
  5. Bank A/c Dr. 1,25,000 (25,000 × ₹5); To Equity Share Application A/c 1,25,000 (directors' subscription).
  6. Equity Share Application A/c Dr. 1,25,000; To Equity Share Capital A/c 1,25,000 (application money transferred).
  7. Loan A/c Dr. 2,50,000; To Bank A/c 2,50,000 (loan repaid from cash proceeds).
  8. Capital Reduction A/c Dr. 7,68,750; To Profit and Loss A/c 5,63,750; To Plant A/c 43,750; To Trademarks and Goodwill A/c (balancing figure) 1,61,250 (losses/assets written off).

Balance Sheet of Star Ltd. (and reduced) as on 31.3.2025: Share Capital 15,12,500 (75,000 6% Pref. shares of ₹10 = 7,50,000 + 1,52,500 Equity shares of ₹5 = 7,62,500); Long-term borrowings 2,78,750 (7,16,250−1,87,500−2,50,000); Trade Payables 2,58,750; Other Liabilities 43,750; Total 20,93,750. Assets: PPE 7,91,250 (Building 5,00,000 + Plant 2,91,250 after the 43,750 write-off); Intangible assets (Trademarks/goodwill) 2,36,250; Inventories 5,00,000; Trade Receivables 4,10,000; Cash 1,56,250 (2,81,250+1,25,000−2,50,000); Total 20,93,750.

Author’s Note: Some examinees missed one of the several simultaneous share issues (rights issue, preference-conversion bonus shares, lender-conversion shares, director subscription), producing an incomplete post-reconstruction share capital figure.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.
