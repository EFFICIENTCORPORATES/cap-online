RTP January 2026 · Q14 · Marks: Not stated in source · Approx Time: 10–20 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U4 : AS 18 — Related Party Disclosures / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 4.6
Question:

Mr. Rajiv, a KMP of Raijada Limited, received a cheque of ₹70,000 towards reimbursement of expenses incurred on hotel stay, conference expenses, etc. while attending a meeting on behalf of the company. Is this transaction covered under AS-18?

Answer / Solution:

Mr. Rajiv, as a KMP, is a related party of Raijada Limited under AS 18. However, a reimbursement of expenses actually incurred on the company's behalf is not in the nature of remuneration or a related-party transaction requiring disclosure — it is simply the company bearing its own cost, routed through the KMP. Hence, this reimbursement need not be disclosed as a related-party transaction under AS 18.

Author’s Note: Some examinees concluded disclosure was required simply because Mr. Rajiv is a related party, without separately testing whether the specific transaction (a cost reimbursement, not remuneration) actually falls within AS 18's disclosure scope.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

RTP September 2025 · Q18 · Marks: Not stated in source · Approx Time: 10–20 minutes (approx.) · M1_C4_U4 : AS 18 — Related Party Disclosures / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 4.4
Question:

X Ltd. sold goods to its associate company for the 1st quarter ending 30.6.2024, priced as it would for any ordinary customer. After that quarter, the related party relationship ceased to exist. Decide whether transactions for the entire year have to be disclosed as related party transactions.

Answer / Solution:

No. Per AS 18 para 23, only the transactions during the period the related party relationship actually existed — here, the first quarter ending 30.6.2024 — need be disclosed as related party transactions. Transactions in the remaining periods, after the relationship ceased, need not be disclosed as such.

Author’s Note: Some examinees required disclosure for the whole year merely because a related party relationship existed at some point during it, not applying the period-specific test AS 18 actually uses.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.
