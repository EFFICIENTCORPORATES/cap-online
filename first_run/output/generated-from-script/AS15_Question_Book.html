MTP January 2026 Set 1 · Q4(a) · Marks: 6 · Approx Time: 11 minutes (approx.) · M2_C6_U1 : AS 15 — Employee Benefits / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.4
Question:

Mr. Suresh works for Raina Ltd. Annual salary ₹30,00,000 in both 2023-24 and 2024-25; 300 working days each year; leave allowed 10 days each year; leave taken 7 days (2023-24) and 13 days (2024-25); leave carried forward to next year: 3 days (2023-24), Nil (2024-25). Raina Ltd. assumes Mr. Suresh will avail the unutilized 3 days of 2023-24 in 2024-25, and contends it will record ₹30,00,000 as employee benefit expense in each year. Comment on the accounting treatment and pass journal entries for both years.

Answer / Solution:

Salary cost per day = ₹30,00,000 / 300 = ₹10,000/day.

Particulars2023-242024-25
Annual salary30,00,00030,00,000
Leave carried forward/(utilised)3 days(3 days)
Expense to be recognised30,30,00029,70,000

Raina Ltd.'s contention (₹30,00,000 flat in both years) is not correct under AS 15 — the accumulating compensated absence for the unused 3 days of 2023-24 must be recognised as an additional expense/provision in that year itself (when earned), not spread evenly.

Journal entries: 2023-24: Employee benefit expense Dr. 30,30,000; To Cash 30,00,000; To Provision for Leave Encashment 30,000. 2024-25: Provision for Leave Encashment Dr. 30,000; Employee benefit expense Dr. 29,70,000; To Cash 30,00,000.

Author’s Note: Some examinees accepted the company's flat ₹30,00,000-per-year treatment without challenging it, missing that AS 15 requires the accumulating leave benefit to be recognised as a provision in the period it is earned, regardless of when it is expected to be used.Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

MTP January 2026 Set 2 · Q6(c) · Marks: 5 · Approx Time: 9 minutes (approx.) · M2_C6_U1 : AS 15 — Employee Benefits / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.9
Question:

Pendora Ltd.'s employee benefit pension plan: fair value of plan assets 01-04-2024 ₹5,00,000; benefits paid 30-11-2024 ₹63,000; inward contributions received 30-09-2024 ₹1,42,000; fair value of plan assets 31-03-2025 ₹7,50,000. Estimates made 01.04.2024: interest and dividend income (after tax) 10.50%; realised gains 2.00%; fund administrative costs (2.00)%; expected rate of annual return 10.50% (compounded annually). Find the expected and actual returns on plan assets as on 31.03.2025 as per AS 15.

Answer / Solution:

Expected return on plan assets: Return on opening ₹5,00,000 held 12 months @10.50% = ₹52,500. Return on ₹1,42,000 contribution held 6 months (from 30.9.24 to 31.3.25) @10.50% = ₹7,455. Less: loss of interest on benefits paid (₹63,000 for the 4 months it was no longer earning return, 30.11.24–31.3.25) @10.50% = (₹2,205). Expected return for 2024-25 = ₹57,750.

Actual return on plan assets: Fair value 31.3.25 (7,50,000) less fair value 1.4.24 (5,00,000) less contributions received (1,42,000) = 1,08,000; add back benefits paid (63,000, since paying out benefits reduces the fair value but isn't a return-related reduction) = Actual return ₹1,71,000.

Author’s Note: Some examinees computed expected return on the full-year balances without time-weighting the mid-year contribution and benefit payment, or confused expected return (an estimate based on opening assumptions) with actual return (derived from the actual movement in fair value).Written by the author, not ICAI — a likely pitfall, not an official finding. May not apply in every case.

PYQ May 2026 · Q1(a) · Marks: 5 · Approx Time: 9 minutes (approx.) · M2_C6_U1 : AS 15 — Employee Benefits / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.10 Compulsory
Question:

Mr. A has joined a company XYZ Ltd. on 1st April, 2025. The terms of his appointment: (i) Mr. A will get ₹18,62,764 as annual emoluments. (ii) The salary of Mr. A is expected to grow @10% per annum. The company also has a policy of giving lump sum payment of 30% of the last drawn annual salary of the employee for each completed year of service if the employee retires after completing minimum 5 years of service. Since the company has inducted Mr. A in the beginning of the year and it is expected that he will complete the minimum five-year term before retiring, he will get 5 yearly increments.

You are required to: (1) Compute the amount that should be charged by XYZ Limited in its Profit & Loss account every year as cost for the Defined Benefit Obligation. (ii) Calculate the current service cost and the interest cost to be charged per year assuming the discount rate is 8% p.a. (P.V. factor for 8% - 0.735, 0.794, 0.857, 0.926, 1) (5 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

(i) Calculation of Defined Benefit Obligation (DBO): Expected last drawn salary = ₹18,62,764 × 110%5 = ₹30,00,000. DBO = ₹30,00,000 × 30% × 5 = ₹45,00,000. Amount of ₹9,00,000 will be charged to Profit and Loss Account every year as cost for the Defined Benefit Obligation.
