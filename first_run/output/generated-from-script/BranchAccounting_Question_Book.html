MTP January 2026 Set 2 · Q2(b) · Marks: 7 · Approx Time: 13 minutes (approx.) · M3_C15_U0 : Accounting for Branches including Foreign Branches / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 1.2
Question:

Care, a retail business, opened a branch Omega on 1st January, 2025; all branch sales on credit. Goods supplied from Head Office, invoiced at 10% above cost. Transactions (Jan/Feb/March 2025): Goods sent to Branch (purchase price) 60,000 / 75,000 / 90,000; Sales as per branch monthly report 57,000 / 63,000 / 82,500; Cash received from debtors and remitted to HO 30,000 / 76,500 / 52,500; Returns to HO (invoice price) 1,800 / 900 / 3,600. Branch closing stock at 31st March, 2025: ₹80,100 at invoice-to-branch price. Record in HO books, showing balances as on 31st March, 2025 and the branch gross profit for the three months.

Answer / Solution:

Branch Account (books of Care, Head Office): To Goods sent to Branch A/c (2,25,000 total invoice value: 60,000+75,000+90,000, at 10% above cost) 2,47,500; To Goods sent to Branch A/c — returns loading (6,300 × 10/110) 573; To Stock Reserve (80,100 × 10/110) 7,283; To Net Profit (balancing figure, transferred to General P&L) 56,045. By Goods sent to Branch — Loading 22,500; By Goods sent to Branch — Returns 6,300; By Bank (cash remitted, 30,000+76,500+52,500) 1,59,000; By Balance c/d — Closing Branch Assets: Stock 80,100, Debtors (Sales 2,02,500 − Collections 1,59,000 = 43,500) 1,23,600. Total both sides ₹3,11,400.

Memorandum Branch Debtors Account confirms Debtors closing balance ₹43,500 (Sales 2,02,500 − Cash received 1,59,000).

Branch gross profit for the three months: ₹56,045 (the loading on goods sent, net of returns and closing stock reserve, represents the branch's realised gross margin for the period).

PYQ May 2026 · Q6(c) · Marks: 6 · Approx Time: 11 minutes (approx.) · M3_C15_U0 : Accounting for Branches including Foreign Branches / ICAI Study Mat Topic No : 7
Question:

(i) Why goods are marked on invoice price by the head office while sending goods to the branch?

(ii) Rama Ltd. has a branch at Delhi, that closes its books of accounts every year on 31st March. This is an independent branch, which maintains comprehensive books of accounts for recording their transactions. You are required to show Journal Entries in the books of branch on 31st March, 2026 to rectify or adjust the following: (A) Head Office allocates ₹2,70,000 to the Branch as Head Office Expenses, not yet recorded by Branch. (B) Depreciation of Branch Fixed Assets, accounts kept by Head Office, not yet recorded in Branch Books ₹2,30,000. (C) Branch paid ₹2,80,000 as salary to a Head Office manager, debited by the branch to its Salaries A/c. (D) Head Office collected ₹2,60,000 directly from a Branch Customer on behalf of the Branch; intimation received by the branch only now, not recorded till now. (E) A remittance of ₹3,00,000 sent by the Branch has not yet been received by Head Office. (F) The Branch incurred Advertisement Expenses of ₹60,000 on behalf of another Branch. (G) Goods worth ₹1,50,000 dispatched by the head office, but the branch has received goods worth ₹1,10,000 till date of reconciliation; rest received subsequently. (H) Goods worth ₹25,000 returned by the branch to head office yet not received by the head office. (6 Marks)

Answer / Solution:

(i) Goods are marked on invoice price to achieve the following objectives: (a) to keep secret from the branch manager the cost price of the goods and profit made, so that the branch manager may not start a rival and competitive business with the concern; (b) to have effective control on stock, i.e. stock at any time must equal opening stock plus goods received from head office minus sales made at branch; (c) to dictate pricing policy to its branches; (d) to save work at branch because prices have already been decided.
