  1. ₹28,00,000
  2. ₹26,00,000
  3. ₹41,00,000
  4. ₹29,00,000

Answer: (D) ₹29,00,000 — the grant was credited to (deducted from) the cost of the Machine per AS 12's Method I, so the depreciable amount is ₹50,00,000 − ₹15,00,000 (grant) − ₹5,00,000 (residual value) = ₹30,00,000 over a 10-year life, i.e. ₹3,00,000 p.a. on the straight-line basis. Answer: (A) ₹4,87,500 — on refund of a grant related to a specific asset, AS 12 requires the book value of the asset to be increased by the refunded amount, with depreciation on the revised book value provided prospectively over its residual useful life. Market value of these investments on 31st March, 2024 is ₹24 Lakhs. Identify the correct statement, considering the above facts as per AS 16:

  2. Since project is qualifying Asset, directors of Ace Limited correctly added the amount of interest in cost of investments.
