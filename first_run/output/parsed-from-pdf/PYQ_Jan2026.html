PYQ Jan 2026 · Part I – Case Scenario I · Q4 · Marks: 2

Determine the value of inventory of unsold finished toys lying at the works of P Limited as at 31st March, 2025 as per AS 2?

  1. ₹30,25,000
  2. ₹29,97,500
  3. ₹32,08,260
  4. ₹28,60,000

Answer: (B) ₹29,97,500 — unit variable cost ₹260 (₹200 material + ₹40 labour + ₹20 variable overhead) plus fixed overhead absorbed at the actual-production rate: since actual production (1,20,000 units) exceeds normal capacity (1,00,000 units), AS 2 requires the fixed-overhead allocation rate to be based on the actual (higher) output in periods of abnormally high production, so that inventory is not carried above cost — ₹15,00,000 ÷ 1,20,000 = ₹12.50/unit, total ₹272.50/unit × 11,000 units = ₹29,97,500.

Using the normal-capacity rate here overstates the closing inventory (giving ₹30,25,000, option A — the distractor).

PYQ Jan 2026 · Part I – Case Scenario II · Q7 · Marks: 2

What would be the carrying amount of Investment in M Limited in the Consolidated Financial Statements of V Limited as at 31st March, 2025?

  1. ₹24,20,000/-
  2. ₹26,00,000/-
  3. ₹18,50,000/-
  4. ₹20,00,000/-

Answer: (B) ₹26,00,000 — under the equity method: cost ₹20,00,000, plus V Limited's 30% share of M Limited's post-acquisition-period profit (M's FY2024-25 profit of ₹30,00,000 accrues evenly over the year; V held the investment for 10 of the 12 months, 1st June 2024 to 31st March 2025, so the relevant share is ₹30,00,000 × 10/12 × 30% = ₹7,50,000), less the dividend received during the holding period (the ₹5,00,000 dividend declared 14th August 2024, of which V's 30% share = ₹1,50,000). Total: ₹20,00,000 + ₹7,50,000 − ₹1,50,000 = ₹26,00,000. The dividend reduces the carrying amount either way under the equity method — unlike the cost method, whether it was declared out of pre- or post-acquisition profit does not change this: the equity method already picks up the investor's share of the investee's profit as it is earned, so a dividend received is simply a cash realisation of value already reflected in the carrying amount, and always reduces it. (The later 15th June 2025 dividend is after the balance sheet date and irrelevant here.)

AS 23 (2.6)
Several examinees either omitted the dividend adjustment entirely, or added it rather than deducting it on the mistaken belief that a dividend from pre-acquisition profits (or one declared after the acquisition date) should be treated as income rather than a reduction of the carrying amount — under the equity method, dividends received always reduce carrying value, regardless of which period's profit funded them.

What is the initial disclosure information of AS-24 for discontinuing operations? (4 Marks)

An enterprise should include the following information relating to a discontinuing operation in its financial statements beginning with the financial statements for the period in which the initial disclosure event occurs: (a) A description of the discontinuing operation(s).
