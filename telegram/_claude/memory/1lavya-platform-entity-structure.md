For the `telegram/` bot suite specifically (Study Hub / Exam Hub / MyFiles Hub and
whatever white-label bots follow), the owning entity is **1LAVYA PVT LTD** — a
company, separate from any individual faculty. **CA Pranav Pratik Tulshyan is
faculty #1 onboarded onto the platform**, not the platform's owner. He currently
teaches Advanced Accounts to CA Inter; may expand to CA Foundation and/or CA Final
later.

**Why this matters:** talk about the bot platform from 1LAVYA's perspective — "1LAVYA
licenses X to faculty," "1LAVYA's exclusive content pool," "faculty pays 1LAVYA" — not
"Pranav's bot." Don't conflate this with the rest of the repo: CLAUDE.md's top-level
framing ("this repo is CA Pranav's CA Inter teaching ecosystem for VC Gurukul") still
describes the books/content pillars (Strategy Book, Concept Book, Question Bank Book,
etc.) correctly — that framing does not apply to the telegram bot platform, which has
its own, different ownership structure.
